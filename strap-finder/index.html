    How to measure lug width

    Grab a caliper or a ruler marked in millimeters. Measure the gap on the inside of the lugs, right where the strap goes in. Round to the nearest whole millimeter. Most watches land on an even number like 18, 20 or 22, but plenty of modern watches use odd sizes like 19 or 21, so do not assume.
