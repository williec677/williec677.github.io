**MrTicTocTalk Journal** is the long form companion to the [MrTicTocTalk Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mrtictoctalk/). Same motto: *Talking Time. One Watch at a Time.*

I'm Will, a Rolex collector. My own rotation includes a Sea Dweller 126600, a Datejust 36 with a black diamond dial, a Yacht Master 37 in Everose with the chocolate dial, and a GMT Master II 16710 in Coke. The one still on the wish list is a steel Sky Dweller.

## What you'll find here

* **Buying guides** for vintage and neo vintage references, focused on the details that change value: dials, hands, bezels, movements, and service history.
* **Reference histories** that separate documented facts from forum folklore.
* **Fake and franken spotting** notes, because the most expensive watch is the wrong one.

## How prices are handled

Asking prices are wishes. Sold prices are facts. When an article mentions a price, it comes with a source and a date, and it reflects a completed sale or a published auction result. If a verified number is not available, the article leaves price out rather than guessing. Markets move, so always check current sold data before you buy.

## How articles are made

Articles are researched against published reference sources, auction house notes, and brand archives, and the key sources are linked inside each post. Drafting is AI assisted, and every article is checked for technical accuracy before it is published. If you spot an error, send a DM on Instagram and it will be corrected.

## Not affiliated

This is an independent collector site. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Rolex, Tudor, Omega, Seiko, or any other brand. Model names are used only to identify the watches being discussed.
