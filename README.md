# MrTicTocTalk Journal

Vintage watch buying guides from the collector behind [@mrtictoctalk](https://www.instagram.com/mrtictoctalk/). Live at https://mrtictoctalk.com

Built with Jekyll on GitHub Pages. See [SETUP.md](SETUP.md) for how to add affiliate IDs, AdSense, and the newsletter from your phone.

Rolex, Omega, Tudor, Seiko and other brand names are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify products. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by any watch brand.

## Collector platform

The Journal keeps its existing article permalinks. Three directories define the collector experience:

- `tools/` holds standalone lookups and the ownership calculator. Calculator inputs stay in memory. Its net USD cost is purchase plus one-time expenses plus annual expenses over the holding period, less expected resale proceeds.
- `collector-vault/` is a browser-local prototype. Its script stores one versioned record per watch, keyed by a stable ID and scoped to the site’s base path. Purchase costs, cumulative expenses and optional manual valuations use integer USD cents. Missing estimates are excluded from the valuation subtotal and its coverage is displayed. Clearing site storage deletes the register.
- `market-intelligence/` brings together published Journal research and existing reference tools. It uses the site's own categories and tags.

Historical valuations and watch comparisons can build on dated observations keyed by watch ID. Service and warranty reminders belong with ownership records. Any future price alerts need a permitted data source and user notification preferences. Membership tiers, cross-device accounts and billing remain future decisions. No external market data is required for the preview.
