The micro brand that made its name on candy colored chronographs just grew up a little, and it did it without losing the jokes. **Studio Underd0g** dropped the **04SERIES Calendar** this morning, its first automatic and its first calendar complication, and the headline feature is a dial that literally counts down to the weekend. Over in Switzerland, **Brellum** shrank its triple calendar moonphase chronograph into a 39 mm case for a 46 piece blue edition, and independent watchmaker **Raúl Pagès** made just three examples of his RP2 for Singapore retailer The Hour Glass. Specs and prices come from the brands and from Hodinkee, Monochrome, Fratello, Time+Tide and aBlogtoWatch, all checked on November 13, 2026.

## Studio Underd0g 04SERIES Calendar: the watch that knows when the weekend starts

Founded by Richard Benc in 2020, Studio Underd0g built its following on three families: the Seagull powered 01SERIES chronographs, the 02SERIES field watches and the Sellita powered 03SERIES monopushers. [Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/studio-underd0g-04series-calendar) points out that this is the first time the brand has stepped outside three handers and chronographs, and [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/studio-underd0g-04series-calendar-collection-retrograde-date-weekend-indicator-new-seagull-movement-value-proposition-introducing-price/) adds that it is also the first Underd0g with an automatic movement.

Here is how the dial works. A retrograde pointer hand sweeps through the dates around the outside of the dial, then snaps back to the 1st when the month ends. Behind a ring of small CNC cut windows sits a rotating disc with colored sections that line up with that month's Saturdays and Sundays, so you can see at a glance how far away the weekend is. The moonphase lives at 6 o'clock, and the retrograde layout is the clever part: a normal sweeping date hand would pass over the moon and hide it once a month, while this one never gets in the way.

One thing to know before you buy: the weekend display is not a mechanical agenda that works out the calendar for you. Monochrome explains that you set it by hand at the start of each month with the crown. The date and moonphase each have their own recessed pusher, one on each side of the case, and [Time+Tide](https://timeandtidewatches.com/studio-underd0g-04series-hands-on/) says the box includes a stylus to press them.

| Dials | Cloud10 (light silvery blue), Arabian Sky (gold), Shepherd's Delight (red), 0vercast (dark blue grey) |

| Assembly | Great Britain, at the brand's D0ghouse workshop in Maidenhead |
| Price | **$1,100 USD** (GBP 750, EUR 800, CHF 800) |
| Availability | Not limited, on sale November 17, 2026 at underd0g.com |

The USD price is Studio Underd0g's own as reported by Hodinkee, Monochrome and Time+Tide.

The movement story matters. Time+Tide reports that Underd0g and Seagull built a new module on top of Seagull's ST-2100 day date base, borrowing parts from the ST-2108 and adding 34 new components to create the ST-2115B, and it keeps the black baseplate look of the 01SERIES calibers. Monochrome notes the watches are assembled, inspected and regulated in Britain.

### My take

This is the most fun way to spend $1,100 on a mechanical watch this month. Retrograde date, moonphase and a weekend countdown at this price is a genuinely new combination, and the two case sizes with the same 44.5 mm lug to lug mean almost any wrist can wear one. The tradeoff is real: the weekend ring needs a monthly reset and the pushers need a stylus, so this is a watch for people who enjoy fiddling with their watches. If that sounds like you, go Cloud10 for everyday wear or Shepherd's Delight if you want everyone to ask what you are wearing. Because it is not limited, there is no reason to panic buy on day one, but the first batch of any hyped Underd0g tends to move fast.

## Brellum Duobox 39 TC Moonphase Chronometer: a full calendar chronograph in a 39 mm case

Brellum is the small Swiss brand founded in 2016 by fourth generation watchmaker Sébastien Muller, and its Duobox gets its name from the two box shaped sapphire crystals, one over the dial and one over the movement. The triple calendar moonphase version used to live only in the bigger 41.8 mm case. The new blue edition brings it down to the 39 mm Duobox 39 case.

The dial is busy in the best vintage calendar way: day and month windows at 12 inside the 30 minute counter, a central hand pointing to the date around the edge, a 6 o'clock subdial that combines the 12 hour counter with the moonphase, and a day and night indicator at 9. Fratello's Jorg Weppelink calls the blue and silver combination winning, and he prefers this smaller case to the bigger versions.

### My take

A COSC chronograph with a full calendar and moonphase for around $4,300 is a strong value play from a brand that makes no more than 299 watches a year, according to [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/brellum-duobox-39-tc-moonphase-chronometer-full-calendar-chronograph-review-price/). Just know the 15.2 mm height is real, and most of it is that domed sapphire. If you want a calendar chronograph that wears like a vintage Valjoux piece without vintage service headaches, this is one of the few options at this price, and 46 pieces will not last long.

## Raúl Pagès RP2 The Hour Glass Edition: three pieces of quiet perfection

Raúl Pagès trained in Michel Parmigiani's restoration workshop and opened his own workshop in Les Brenets in 2012. His RP1 won the Louis Vuitton Watch Prize for Independent Creatives in 2024, and the RP2 followed in 2025 as the more classical three hander that anchors his collection. For The Hour Glass, he swapped the standard cerulean blue dial for a German silver base, a black nickel plated hour ring and a light blue mother of pearl center, with a grey recessed small seconds at 6.

The case stays 38.5 mm by 11.5 mm in steel, water resistant to 30 m. The hand wound caliber beats at 21,600 vph with a 60 hour reserve and a large 12.5 mm variable inertia balance. For this edition the bridges are frosted with polished bevels instead of the standard Geneva stripes. [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/raul-pages-rp2-the-hour-glass-edition-independent-watchmaking-introducing-price/) reports it comes on two leather straps and is limited to **three pieces**.

Price is on request. Monochrome lists the regular RP2 at CHF 89,000 (about $106,200 USD) and estimates this edition will land closer to CHF 100,000 (about $119,300 USD); that second number is Monochrome's estimate, not an official price.

### My take

Three pieces at a single retailer means almost nobody reading this will get one, but it is a great signal of where independent watchmaking is right now: steel case, six figure price, and collectors happy to pay it for the finishing alone.
