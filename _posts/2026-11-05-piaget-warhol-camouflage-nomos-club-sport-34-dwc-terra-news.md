## Piaget Andy Warhol Camouflage: 27 pieces of stone and gold, 86 watches

Some history first, because it matters here. Andy Warhol owned seven Piaget watches. One of them was a 45mm cushion cased, gadrooned reference 15102 designed by Jean-Claude Gueit, which he bought in 1973. Collectors called it the Black Tie, and in 2024 Piaget officially renamed it the Andy Warhol Watch with the Warhol Foundation's blessing ([Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/piaget-andy-warhol-camouflage-limited-edition-review-price/)). After the Clou de Paris in 2024 and last year's Collage, the Camouflage is the newest chapter.

The dial is the reason to care. Piaget took Warhol's 1986 camouflage self portraits and rebuilt the pattern in marquetry, using deep blue sodalite, bright turquoise, satin finished yellow gold and polished rose gold. That's 27 individually cut pieces in total, and because stone and metal sit at different textures, the pattern has real depth instead of looking printed. Polished dauphine hands stay out of the way so the artwork does the talking.

The case is 18k white gold with a rhodium finish, 45 x 43mm and only 8.08mm thick, keeping the stepped gadroons and recessed crown. Inside is Piaget's in house automatic calibre 501P1, based on the ultra thin 430P, at 3.63mm thick, 28,800 vph and a 40 hour power reserve. The solid caseback carries Warhol's signature and the edition number, water resistance is 30m, and it ships on a blue alligator strap with a pin buckle ([Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/piaget-andy-warhol-camouflage-limited-edition-review-price/), [SJX](https://watchesbysjx.com/2026/11/piaget-andy-warhol-camouflage.html)).

**Reference and price:** G0A51242, limited to 86 pieces as a nod to 1986. Monochrome and Luxe Digital report a price of CHF 74,000, while Piaget's own [US product page](https://www.piaget.com/us-en/watches/andy-warhol/automatic-white-gold-turquoise-sodalite-watch-g0a51242) lists it as price on request, so there's no official US dollar figure yet.

My take? This is art you wear to dinner, not a daily driver, and the movement is solid rather than exciting, a point SJX makes too. But a hand cut stone marquetry dial on a case this thin is rare craft, and with only 86 made, that rarity is the whole point. ## Nomos Club Sport neomatik 34 All Silver: the quiet one with 200 meters

Nomos added a 34mm size to the Club Sport line in 2024 with rosé, gold and purple dials. The new All Silver drops the color completely. You get a silver plated sunburst dial, a recessed small seconds with a finer circular finish, rhodium plated hands, and white Super-LumiNova that glows blue at night. The only splash of gold is the "neomatik" script ([Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/nomos-club-sport-neomatik-34-all-silver-review-price/)).

The specs are what make this one interesting. Per [Nomos](https://nomos-glashuette.com/en-us/club/club-sport-neomatik-34-all-silver-758), the polished steel case measures 34mm across, 8.2mm thick and 41.5mm lug to lug, with a screw down crown, domed sapphire crystal and 20 atm of water resistance, so you can really swim with it. Nomos's red ring on the crown warns you when it isn't screwed down. Power comes from the in house DUW 3001 automatic, just 3.2mm thick, beating at 21,600 vph with the Nomos swing system and Glashütte three quarter plate. Monochrome's write up gives two power reserve figures, 42 and 43 hours, so call it roughly 42 hours. It sits on the integrated Club Sport bracelet with quick change spring bars, a deployant clasp and an 18mm lug width.

**References and price:** Ref. 758 with a sapphire caseback is USD 3,880 and Ref. 758.SB with a solid steel caseback is USD 3,490 on the Nomos US store ([758](https://nomos-glashuette.com/en-us/club/club-sport-neomatik-34-all-silver-758), [758.SB](https://nomos-glashuette.com/en-us/club/club-sport-neomatik-34-all-silver-758.sb)). In Europe it's EUR 3,020 and EUR 2,720. It joins the regular collection, so it isn't a limited edition.

My take? Thin, 200 meters, an in house movement, and a bracelet that fits a smaller wrist. Few watches at this price check all four boxes. If you've been looking at a 36mm sports watch and finding it too big, this is the one to try on. The solid back version saves you $390, and I'd take that deal, since the case flip is the only real difference. Want to see how Nomos holds value? ## DWC Terra: the $48 carabiner watch is coming back

If your feed was full of a flat, lugless steel brick clipped to backpacks this year, that was the DWC Terra. Fratello took a deep look today at how a watch from the Delhi Watch Company, a brand started in New Delhi in 2020, became one of the most viral releases of the year ([Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/dwc-terra-the-e42-carabiner-watch-that-broke-the-internet/)).

The facts, per Fratello: the Terra launched in late 2025 for DWC's fifth anniversary at ₹4,499, about €42 or US$48. You get a 316L steel case in sandblasted, vertically brushed or black DLC, 32mm wide, 44mm long and about 9.6mm thick, with domed Schott glass, a screw in caseback, 50m of water resistance and a Miyota GL32 quartz movement. The case has angled slots instead of lugs, so it takes pass through straps up to about 22mm, ships on a 20mm parachute strap, and clips onto anything that fits the channel.

The hype is the story. Fratello reports the first batch of 1,700 sold out in under two minutes, a second batch sold out just as fast in spring 2026, and a 30,000 piece preorder window in July sold out too. Fratello also says DWC didn't ship to the US because of tariffs, and that listed resale prices climbed as high as €500.

**When it returns:** DWC cofounder Anish Dandwani told Fratello the Terra will likely return in early 2027, with March as the official target and a slim chance of February depending on production. A version 2 based on owner feedback may follow around April or May. Those are the brand's plans as reported by Fratello, not locked dates, and US availability hasn't been confirmed, so treat it as expected, not guaranteed.

My take? Don't pay €500 for a $48 quartz watch. Sign up with DWC and wait for March.
