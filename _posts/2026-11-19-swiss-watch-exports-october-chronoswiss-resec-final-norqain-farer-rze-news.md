Big day for the business side of watches. The Federation of the Swiss Watch Industry just dropped its October numbers, and on paper they look great: a fifth straight month of growth and a massive jump in shipments to the United States. Read the fine print, though, and the federation itself is telling everyone to stay calm. After the numbers we have four fresh releases with verified US prices, from a 100 piece Chronoswiss farewell to a $299 USD solar field watch. Let's go.

## Swiss watch exports rise 9.1% in October

**Published:** November 19, 2026 ([FH press release, PDF](https://www.fhs.swiss/eng/statistics/pdf/comm_261010_a.pdf), [FH statistics](https://www.fhs.swiss/eng/statistics), [Rapaport](https://rapaport.com/news/swiss-watch-exports-continue-to-climb-in-october/), [WWD](https://wwd.com/accessories-news/watches/swiss-watch-exports-october-growth-streak-job-cuts-1239426981/))

Here are the headline figures straight from the FH:

* **Total exports:** CHF 2,456.4 million in October, about $2.92 billion USD, up 9.1% on October 2025. That is the fifth consecutive month of growth.
* **Wristwatches:** 1.55 million units (up 12.9%) worth CHF 2,343.8 million, about $2.78 billion USD (up 9.5%). The FH says that is almost 177,000 more watches than a year earlier.
* **January through October:** close to CHF 22 billion, about $26.1 billion USD, up 3.4% on the same period last year.

### The US number is huge, but look at the base

Exports to the United States hit CHF 363.7 million in October, about $432 million USD, up a monster 60.8%. The FH is upfront that this is still driven by a favorable base effect linked to US tariffs: October 2025 was only CHF 226.1 million. The more sobering comparison is October 2024, when the US took CHF 421.7 million, so this October is still 13.8% below where it was two years ago, per the FH market table. Over the first ten months of 2026, exports to the US are down 0.1% versus 2025, but 3.6% higher than 2024.

### Who is up and who is down

* **France:** up 101.6% to CHF 239.8 million, but the FH says that spike is mainly re exports to other markets. The European Union as a whole grew 9.2%, which the FH calls a closer reflection of the real trend.
* **Hong Kong:** up 7.3% to CHF 178.2 million.
* **United Kingdom:** up 6.5% to CHF 163.1 million.
* **South Korea:** up 22.8%.
* **Japan:** down 2.1% to CHF 177.0 million.
* **China:** down 12.1% to CHF 166.9 million.
* **Singapore:** down 14.4%. **UAE:** down 3.2%.

### Steel and two tone did the heavy lifting

By material, steel watches grew 11.2% in value, bimetallic (gold and steel) jumped 28.3%, and the other metals category rose 31.0%. Precious metal watches were basically flat at plus 0.4%. Every price segment grew: watches with an export price above CHF 3,000 (about $3,560 USD) rose 9.0%, the mid segment rose 6.6%, and watches under CHF 500 (about $593 USD) rose 23.5%.

### The catch

This is the part I want you to remember. The FH itself warns that the figures "should be interpreted with caution" because of higher gold prices, the strong Swiss franc and US tariffs, all of which squeeze margins, and it says export growth "is therefore not, in itself, a comprehensive indicator of the sector's economic performance." Also remember these are exports to markets, not sales to people like you and me. So no, this does not mean your dealer is about to discount, and it does not mean prices are coming down. If anything, gold heavy two tone growing 28.3% in value is a reminder of how expensive gold has become. The next FH release, covering November, is scheduled for December 17, 2026.

## Also new: Chronoswiss ReSec Final Edition

**Announced:** November 19, 2026 ([Chronoswiss US](https://chronoswiss.com/en/us/resec-final-edition-manufacture-CH-6923T.1-BRAN), [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/introducing-chronoswiss-resec-final-edition-last-of-modern-regulator-guilloche-dial-price/), [Time+Tide](https://timeandtidewatches.com/chronoswiss-resec-final-edition-introducing/))

![Chronoswiss ReSec Final Edition in titanium with rose gold CVD hand guilloche turbine dial, grey sandblasted bridges, off center hours at twelve and retrograde seconds at six on a grey rubber strap]({{ "/assets/img/watches/chronoswiss-resec-final-edition.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Chronoswiss (ref. CH-6923T.1-BRAN)*

Curtain call. Chronoswiss launched the Open Gear ReSec in 2018, a regulator with off center hours at twelve, central minutes, retrograde seconds at six and its bridges stacked right on top of the dial. Eight years and a lot of wild colorways later, the brand is closing the chapter with the ReSec Final Edition, ref. CH-6923T.1-BRAN.

* **Case:** 42 mm x 14.2 mm, 51 mm lug to lug, a 17 part grade 5 titanium case with a polished, partly knurled bezel and the signature onion crown, curved sapphire with anti reflective coating on both sides, sapphire display back, 100 m water resistance.
* **Dial:** a hand guilloché turbine pattern made in the Lucerne atelier with a warm rose gold CVD coating, under sandblasted light grey CVD bridges, minute track and retrograde seconds assembly. Ceramic markers glow in a Light Old Radium tone.
* **Movement:** automatic Calibre C.6005, made with La Joux-Perret, 31 jewels, 28,800 vph, 55 hours, with a skeletonized tungsten rotor on ball bearings.
* **Strap:** grey rubber with a folding clasp.
* **Limited:** 100 pieces worldwide.

**Price:** **$17,300 USD** on the Chronoswiss US site (CHF 14,400 in Switzerland). The US page offers a $250 USD reservation ticket that is deducted from the final price, with a $2,500 USD deposit at the allocation stage. One heads up: Time+Tide prints the Swiss price as CHF 4,400, which is a typo; the brand site and Monochrome both say CHF 14,400.

**My take:** the ReSec was always the most modern looking regulator out there, and the rose gold and grey combo is the most grown up version yet. 100 pieces of a farewell edition from a brand with a real cult following? These will not hang around.

## Also new: Norqain Independence Skeleton 40 mm Arctic Blue

**Announced:** November 19, 2026 ([Norqain US](https://norqain.com/products/independence-skeleton?country=US), [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/the-norqain-independence-skeleton-40mm-arctic-blue-with-updated-steel-bracelet/), [Time+Tide](https://timeandtidewatches.com/norqain-independence-arctic-blue-introducing/))

![Norqain Independence Skeleton 40 mm Arctic Blue with translucent blue skeleton dial and redesigned semi integrated steel bracelet]({{ "/assets/img/watches/norqain-independence-skeleton-arctic-blue.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Norqain (ref. N3008.07S01.Q01.S01)*

Norqain keeps the ice theme going. The new Independence Skeleton Arctic Blue, ref. N3008.07S01.Q01.S01, pairs a translucent Arctic blue openworked dial with a redesigned semi integrated steel bracelet with vertically brushed and polished links and micro adjustment. It joins the permanent collection, per Monochrome.

Specs per Norqain: 40 mm x 12.05 mm steel case, 46.3 mm lug to lug, 20 mm between the lugs, flat sapphire front and back, screw down crown, 100 m water resistance. Inside is the COSC certified Calibre N08S, a skeletonized Sellita SW200-1 base, with 26 jewels, 4 Hz and 41 hours of power reserve.

**Price:** **$5,580 USD** on the Norqain US store (CHF 4,300 in Switzerland, EUR 4,650 per Monochrome). Note that Time+Tide lists US$5,772; the brand's own US store shows $5,580 USD, and that is the number we are going with.

**My take:** that blue is basically a glacier in a 40 mm case, and the slimmer bracelet is the real upgrade here. If integrated sports watches are your thing but you want something nobody else at the bar is wearing, this is a fun pick.

## Also new: Farer Field Watch Series III

**Announced:** November 19, 2026 ([Farer US](https://usd.farer.com/products/lomond-midnight), [Time+Tide](https://timeandtidewatches.com/farer-field-watch-series-iii-introducing-2/))

![Farer Lomond Midnight field watch with navy PVD steel case, fully luminous aquamarine dial, navy numerals, pointer date hand and navy sailcloth strap]({{ "/assets/img/watches/farer-lomond-midnight.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Farer (Lomond Midnight)*

Farer just rebuilt its field watch from the case up, and the Midnight models are pure Farer. Four versions:

* **Lomond III:** gravel textured navy dial with custard yellow X1 Super LumiNova markers, **$1,150 USD**.
* **Pembroke III:** fully luminous ivory dial with navy printing, **$1,195 USD**.
* **Lomond Midnight:** navy PVD case with a fully luminous aquamarine dial, **$1,250 USD**.
* **Pembroke Midnight:** navy PVD case with a fully luminous orange dial (Farer calls it orange sorbet), **$1,250 USD**.

Specs per Farer: a new 40 mm x 11.7 mm steel case, just 44 mm lug to lug, 20 mm lugs, flat sapphire with six layers of internal anti reflective coating, screw in crown with a bronze cap, 150 m water resistance, and a pointer date read off a date scale on the sloped chapter ring. The movement is the Swiss Sellita SW221-2 Power+ with about 65 hours of power reserve at 28,800 vph. Every watch ships with a navy sailcloth strap plus a second strap of your choice in leather, suede or rubber.

**Price note:** those are the US store prices on usd.farer.com today. Farer says US tariffs are charged at checkout on top of that and are non refundable, so budget a little extra. UK prices are £995 to £1,095.

**My take:** a 44 mm lug to lug on a 40 mm watch is a gift for smaller wrists, and 65 hours means it survives a full weekend in the drawer. The aquamarine Lomond Midnight is the one that will stop people. ## Also new: RZE Seeker solar field watch

**Announced:** November 2026, on sale now ([RZE](https://rzewatches.com/products/seeker-carbon-black), [aBlogtoWatch](https://www.ablogtowatch.com/hands-on-debut-rze-seeker-solar-field-watch-offers-maximum-autonomy-for-an-entry-level-price/))

![RZE Seeker Carbon Black titanium solar field watch with black sand textured dial, white Arabic numerals and yellow seconds hand on a black nylon strap]({{ "/assets/img/watches/rze-seeker-carbon-black.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: RZE (Seeker Carbon Black)*

Now the value play. The RZE Seeker is a 38 mm titanium solar field watch in Carbon Black, Spruce Green and Metropolis Blue, and aBlogtoWatch calls it the brand's least expensive analog model.

* **Case:** solid titanium with RZE's hardened UltraHex coating, 38 mm wide, 10 mm thick and 45.5 mm lug to lug per RZE (aBlogtoWatch measured 10.2 mm and 45.7 mm), 20 mm lugs, about 45 g on the nylon strap, 100 m water resistance, sapphire with inner anti reflective coating.
* **Movement:** RZE lists a Japan solar movement; aBlogtoWatch identifies it as the Seiko/Epson AS01, running about four months on a full charge.
* **Dial:** sand textured, with Super LumiNova and RZE's signature yellow seconds hand.

**Price:** **$299 USD** on the RZE site, all three colors, with RZE saying US duties and taxes are included in the checkout price where available. Shipping is listed as mid to late December.

**My take:** titanium, sapphire, 100 m and you never wind or change a battery for $299 USD. That is a ridiculous beater watch. If you want the mechanical version of this idea, check our [Hamilton Khaki Field buyer's guide]({% post_url 2026-11-16-hamilton-khaki-field-buyers-guide %}).
