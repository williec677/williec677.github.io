## Klokers KLOK03 Automatic: first mechanical disc watch from EUR 790

Klokers spent a decade proving you can read time without hands. Every model from 2015 through 2025 ran quartz. After bankruptcies in 2020 and 2023, and a rescue by Christian-Louis Col (also owner of Ikepod), the brand is back with its first automatic: the KLOK03 ([Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/2026-new-klokers-klok03-disc-watch-now-goes-automatic-review-price/)).

The signature stacked clockwise discs stay. Hours sit on the lower disc, minutes in the middle, seconds in the center, all read through a magnifier at 6 o'clock. Designer Fabrice Gonet (Edge Design, Ikepod Seaslug, MB&F work) gave the discs tiered depth the flat KLOK01 and KLOK08 never had. Six colorways ship: Yellow Dot, Blue Note, Yuri, Desert Storm, Orange Dot, and Kaki K.

Monochrome's spec table is the clean reference. Case is 316L steel, 40mm across, 13.9mm thick, 45mm lug to lug, sapphire with the Klokers logo, solid caseback, 30m water resistance. Movement is the Miyota 82S0 automatic (21 jewels, 21,600 vph, 42 hour reserve, hacking). Straps are 20mm textile with quick release. The old detachable head system is gone; this is a conventional complete watch. Made in Hong Kong, two year warranty.

Price is EUR 890 including VAT, with a launch price of EUR 790 until December 20, 2026. Available now at [klokers.com](https://www.klokers.com/en/shop/klok-03/klok03-01-yellow-dot-412) with delivery about one week. The product page shows 890.00; Monochrome confirms the launch cut.

My take? Under EUR 900 for a first mechanical Klokers with a real design language is a strong re-entry. 30m is daily wear, not swimming. If you already own a KLOK01 or KLOK08, straps do not cross over. ## Briston Clubmaster Legend Compressor: dual crown dive vibes from EUR 590

Briston's Clubmaster Legend line already covers divers and open hearts under about EUR 600. The new Compressor sub-collection nods at 1950s and 1960s super compressor dual crown watches without claiming a real EPSA style case ([Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/briston-new-clubmaster-legend-compressor-collection-in-steel-and-acetate-accessible-automatic-dive-watch-review-price/)).

Two crowns tell the story. The 2 o'clock crown turns a bidirectional inner bezel. The 4 o'clock crown sets time and date. The cushion case is 42mm across and 14.65mm thick, in either fully polished 316L steel or Italian tortoiseshell cellulose acetate with steel lugs, bezel, crowns, and screw down caseback. Domed K1 crystal is 2.8mm thick. Water resistance is 200m. Dial options are black or navy with green Super-LumiNova C3 on applied indexes and Arabic numerals, a white railway track, a red inner dive track, and a silver white inner bezel with a luminous triangle.

Movement is the Seiko NH35 (21,600 vph, 41 hour reserve, quick date). Strap is matte FKM rubber, 20mm tapering to 18mm, with Briston's presto quick release, in black or navy to match the dial. References: 26642.SA.T.1.FB (black acetate), 26642.SA.T.15.FNB (navy acetate), 26642.PS.DC.1.FB (black steel), 26642.PS.DC.15.FNB (navy steel). Prices confirmed by Monochrome and [briston-watches.com](https://briston-watches.com): EUR 590 acetate, EUR 610 steel.

My take? The acetate is the Briston move. Steel is the safer daily. Either way you get a real 200m automatic cushion with compressor styling under EUR 620. ## Praesidus Montar Ranger Ana-Digi and GMT: outdoor duo at $275

Praesidus, founded in 2019 with a veterans and service inspired brief, just opened the Montar collection for recreational outdoor use rather than hard military cosplay ([Worn & Wound](https://wornandwound.com/introducing-the-praesidus-montar-ranger-ana-digi-and-gmt/)).

**Ranger Ana-Digi.** 44mm matte black stainless steel case and bezel, rotating compass bezel, analog hands plus digital time and date, alarm and stopwatch, sapphire coated mineral crystal, 100m water resistance. Colorways (50 pieces each): Flint, Graphite, Canyon, Cinder. Indices run beige or white depending on the version; Cinder also adds green to the outer minute track.

**Ranger GMT.** 43mm polycarbonate case, field dial with date at 3, 24 hour bezel with large numerals, same 100m rating and sapphire coated mineral. Colorways (50 pieces each): Lichen, Dune, Signal, Basalt. Dial and bezel colors shift more aggressively here; Dune is the bright yellow face, Lichen the muted green, Signal and Basalt the black dials with yellow or green accents.

Both retail $275 USD per [praesidus.com/collections/montar](https://praesidus.com/collections/montar) and Worn & Wound. Casebacks are etched with the water rating, a serial out of 200 (50 per colorway), and the Praesidus logo. Worn & Wound does not name a caliber for either model, so I am not inventing one.

My take? At $275 with 50 piece colorways, these are impulse tool watches for people who actually hike and paddle, not shelf queens. The Ana-Digi is the function play. The GMT is the cleaner look. ## Trafford Watch Co. Daytripper Violet Crown: Austin sunset, 200 pieces at $799

Trafford Watch Co. named this Daytripper special edition after Austin's "City of the Violet Crown" nickname for the purple pink haze at sunset ([Time and Tide](https://timeandtidewatches.com/trafford-violet-crown-introducing/)). The dial runs a blue to purple to pink gradient with Trafford's double brushed texture, polished applied Arabic numerals at 12/3/6/9, a luminous chapter ring with a 24 hour scale, and a thin pink outer band that flashes under the double domed crystal.

Case is familiar Daytripper geometry: 39.5mm diameter, 12mm thick, 46mm lug to lug, 316L steel with a hardening coating, double domed sapphire with inner AR, sapphire exhibition back, 100m water resistance, 20mm lugs. Movement is a modified Miyota 9075 with the GMT hand removed, keeping the independent local hour jump for travel. Trafford claims ±10 seconds regulation and a 42 hour reserve. A pink rotor shows through the back. Strap is 20mm purple Italian leather; optional steel bracelet is +$149 with quick release solid end links and on the fly micro adjust.

Price is US$799 / £725 per the brand product page and Time and Tide's main table. Availability: limited to 200 numbered pieces (brand and the Time and Tide spec table). One Time and Tide shop line says "limited to 20 pieces" for Time+Tide Shop and Discovery Studios in London and New York. Treat that as a small retail allocation, not the total run.

My take? A real hometown dial story under $800 with a travel friendly hour jumper is easy to like. Size is wearable. Confirm stock on [traffordwatchco.com](https://www.traffordwatchco.com/products/daytripper-violet-crown) before you assume a colorway is open.
