The US is mostly asleep over turkey prep, but Europe kept the releases coming. The headline is a vintage style column wheel chronograph from a revived Swiss name, followed by a very sensible Hamilton, a wild new Casio pilot watch and a small Citizen refresh. Here is what matters.

## Lebois & Co Heritage Baton Chronograph and Heritage Sector Chronograph Frosted Forest

**Announced:** November 26, 2026 ([Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/lebois-heritage-baton-chronograph-and-heritage-sector-frosted-forest-chronograph-introducing-price/), [Uhren-Nerd](https://www.uhren-nerd.de/lebois-co-heritage-chronograph-baton-frosted-forest/))

Quick background: Lebois & Co was founded in 1934 by Raymond Dodane, went quiet in 1972, and was revived in 2014 by Dutch entrepreneur Tom van Wijlick, per Monochrome. The Heritage Chronograph started in 2021 as a community project where collectors voted on the case, dial details and movement, and the first watches shipped in 2023. These two new references are part of the brand's 2027 production.

The **Baton** (ref. 324.479) comes from the archive. Lebois bought a vintage chronograph from a private collector with a white to cream dial, and that piece is the template here: tachymeter and telemeter scales, blue Arabic numerals at 12 and 6, baton markers and blue leaf hands. Uhren-Nerd notes it is a sister to the salmon Baton 324.478 that is already in the range.

The **Frosted Forest** (ref. 324.443) has a more personal backstory. Monochrome says van Wijlick met a London collector, Asim Rai, in October 2023, and that meeting led to this edition. It is a brushed green sector dial with a tachymeter only, silver tone details and luminous leaf hands, meant to evoke a forest under frost.

### Specs

* **References:** 324.479 (Baton, cream) and 324.443 (Sector, Frosted Forest green)
* **Case:** stainless steel, 39 mm, brushed and polished, domed sapphire crystal, sapphire display back, 50 m. Uhren-Nerd lists 47.35 mm lug to lug and 20 mm between the lugs.
* **Thickness:** Monochrome gives 10.5 mm; Uhren-Nerd gives 10.5 mm without the crystal and 13.9 mm with the domed crystal, so plan on the larger number for shirt cuffs.
* **Movement:** La Joux-Perret LC-450, hand wound column wheel chronograph, 28,800 vph, about 60 hours. Monochrome lists 24 jewels and Uhren-Nerd 23, so we flag that small gap.
* **Layout:** small seconds and 30 minute counter in sunken, snailed subdials, central chronograph seconds
* **Strap:** brown leather (Baton) or green leather (Frosted Forest) with a steel pin buckle

**Price and availability:** **$4,500 USD** (EUR 3,950) for either reference, per Monochrome. Preorders open November 26, 2026, deliveries are expected in mid 2027. Uhren-Nerd adds that a EUR 1,975 deposit holds a slot in Europe, with the balance due before delivery.

**My take:** a hand wound column wheel chronograph in a 39 mm case under $5,000 is a short list, and Lebois keeps it interesting by giving each dial a real story. If you want something that ships sooner, the [Hanhart 417 C Panna]({% post_url 2026-11-24-hanhart-417-c-panna-flyback-sher-obtuse-news %}) we covered this week is the other vintage flavored chronograph worth comparing.

## Hamilton Jazzmaster Auto 40mm

**Launched:** Hamilton presented it on November 12, 2026 per Uhren-Nerd, and the international press picked it up today ([Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/hamilton-jazzmaster-automatic-40mm-accessible-everyday-watch-review-price/), [Hamilton US product page](https://www.hamiltonwatch.com/en-us/h32435140-jazzmaster-auto.html))

![Three Hamilton Jazzmaster Auto 40mm watches with silver, beige and blue dials on black and brown leather straps]({{ "/assets/img/watches/hamilton-jazzmaster-auto-40mm.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Monochrome Watches*

Not every release needs a backstory. The Jazzmaster Auto 40mm is the closed dial, time and date Jazzmaster that sits below the Open Heart models, and it reads like a smart office watch. The dials (black, blue, silver and beige) use two finishes, a circular satin outer ring and a sunray center, with applied Arabic numerals at 12, 3, 6 and 9, baton indexes and a date at 6.

### Specs

* **Case:** stainless steel, 40 mm, 10 mm thick, polished and brushed, sapphire crystal with anti reflective coating, solid caseback, 50 m, 20 mm lug width
* **Movement:** Hamilton H-10 automatic (Powermatic 80 family), 80 hour power reserve, Nivachron balance spring. Monochrome gives 21,600 vph; Hamilton's product page does not list a frequency.
* **References (per Uhren-Nerd):** bracelet H32435130 black, H32435140 blue, H32435150 silver; leather H32435520 beige, H32435540 blue, H32435730 black, H32435750 silver. The beige dial is leather only.
* **Straps:** three row steel bracelet with folding clasp, or brown or black calf leather with pin buckle; all on Hamilton's EasyClick quick release

**Price:** **$725 USD** on the bracelet (H32435140 shows in stock) and **$675 USD** on leather (H32435540 shows "available soon"), per Hamilton's US site. In Switzerland it is CHF 620 and CHF 550.

**My take:** 80 hours, sapphire, 10 mm thick and a real bracelet for $725 is a strong everyday value, and it fits right into the gift range we mapped in our [watch gifts under $500 USD]({% post_url 2026-11-22-watch-gifts-under-500 %}) guide for anyone stretching the budget a little. Its closest relative is the Tissot Gentleman, which uses the same Powermatic 80 base but takes 21 mm straps; see the [Tissot Gentleman strap page](https://mrtictoctalk.com/strap-finder/tissot-gentleman/). The Jazzmaster is 20 mm, so a {% include aff.html network="amazon" url="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=20mm+quick+release+leather+watch+strap" text="20 mm quick release leather strap" %} is an easy way to dress it up or down.

## Casio G-Shock Gravitymaster GWR-B3000SC-1A

**Launched:** UK first, reported November 26, 2026 ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Casio-drops-a-cool-pilot-watch-with-multi-fiber-strap-and-TOUGH-MVT-2-solar-movement.1460005.0.html), [earlier leak report, Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/A-new-Casio-G-Shock-Gravitymaster-pilot-watch-with-a-fabric-strap-is-on-the-way.1437132.0.html))

![Casio G-Shock Gravitymaster GWR-B3000SC-1A pilot watch with grey and black multi fiber strap in front of aircraft cockpit gauges]({{ "/assets/img/watches/casio-gravitymaster-gwr-b3000sc-1a.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Casio via Notebookcheck*

This is the opposite of the Hamilton. Casio's Master of G pilot line gets a strap developed only for this model, mixing Kevlar, nylon and metal parts, inspired by cockpit seat belts and harnesses, with hook and loop closure, a forged buckle and a yellow pull tab styled after an ejection seat tag.

### Specs (per Notebookcheck, citing Casio)

* **Case:** stainless steel and carbon reinforced resin, black and grey finishes, sapphire crystal with anti reflective coating, 56.7 x 47.3 x 14.1 mm, 104 g, made in Japan
* **Movement:** TOUGH MVT.2, Tough Solar, Bluetooth, Multiband 6 radio sync, automatic hand correction with shock and magnetic field detection
* **Functions:** dual city world time, direct UTC access, flight log through the Casio Watches app
* **Toughness:** Triple G Resist (shock, centrifugal force, vibration), 200 m

**Price:** **£920 in the UK, about $1,202 USD** at the November 25 ECB reference rates (USD 1.0935 and GBP 0.83730 per euro). Casio has not announced US or EU timing, and there is no official USD price yet. A Japanese G-Shock fan blog reported a December Japan release at ¥154,000, which we have not confirmed with Casio Japan.

**My take:** priced like a Swiss automatic, so this one is for G-Shock collectors more than first timers. The everyday G-Shock most people actually wear is still the GA-2100, and our [Casio G-Shock GA-2100 strap page](https://mrtictoctalk.com/strap-finder/casio-g-shock-ga-2100/) covers swaps. For the latest Casioak news, see our [Bell & Ross BR-05 Moonphase roundup]({% post_url 2026-11-25-bell-ross-br-05-moonphase-unimatic-massena-ghost-g-shock-casioak-news %}).

## Citizen Axiom Refresh, AU2000 to AU2006

**Spotted:** November 26, 2026 on Citizen's US store ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/US-launch-Citizen-s-new-slim-minimalist-Eco-Drive-watches-debut-with-8-references-in-tow.1460022.0.html), [Citizen US, AU2000-58F](https://www.citizenwatch.com/us/en/product/AU2000-58F.html))

A short one. Citizen has put eight new Axiom references on its US site with "New" tags, and older models like the AU1062-56E now show sold out, per Notebookcheck. The formula stays the same: 40 mm, edge to edge crystal, black dial, two hands, date at 3, Eco-Drive. The diamond versions add 11 diamonds around the dial. Notebookcheck notes a mineral crystal and splash resistance only, so no swimming.

* **AU2000-58F** steel bracelet, **$350 USD** (confirmed on Citizen US); AU2000-58G with diamonds, $450
* **AU2002-01E** gold tone on black leather, $375; AU2002-52E gold tone bracelet, $425; AU2002-52G with diamonds, $495
* **AU2005-54E** black IP bracelet, $425; AU2005-54G with diamonds, $495
* **AU2006-01E** black IP on black leather, **$375 USD** (confirmed on Citizen US)

One open question: Citizen's US product pages list caliber E175 in the spec table but J165 in the description, so it is not yet clear whether the movement changed.
