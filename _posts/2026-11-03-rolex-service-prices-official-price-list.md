For decades, the Rolex service counter worked like a mystery box. You handed over your watch, waited for an estimate, and hoped. That era is over. Rolex now lists its prices for a complete service right on rolex.com, broken out by model family and by how many digits are in your reference number. Gear Patrol reported the new page on October 29, 2026 ([Gear Patrol](https://www.gearpatrol.com/watches/rolex-service-pricing/)), and Rolex Magazine called it a first for the brand on November 1 ([Rolex Magazine](https://www.rolexmagazine.com/2026/11/)).

I pulled every number below straight from the US page on November 3, 2026 ([Rolex](https://www.rolex.com/en-us/watch-care-and-service/servicing-prices)). If you own a Rolex, or you are about to buy one used, this is the most useful thing the brand has published in years.

## The three tiers, decoded

Rolex sorts everything by the reference number. Six digits means a modern watch, five digits means what Rolex calls a Classic model, and four digits means Vintage. Not sure which you have? Rolex marks the $3,850, $5,500 and $8,810 tiers with a note: only the Rolex Restoration Atelier in Geneva is authorized to work on those references.

## What the price actually buys

For six and five digit watches, the complete service covers movement servicing and a waterproofness test. Rolex lists the parts that are included: screws, springs, wheels, lubricants, acrylic crystals, gaskets and tubes. Repairs to the case and bracelet are not included, other spare parts are billed separately, polishing is optional, and bracelet sizing is free. Estimates for modern and classic watches are free too.

Vintage is a different deal, and you need to read it closely. The four digit service covers the movement only, with wear components "subject to availability." Work on the case, bracelet, dial and hands is excluded. Prices are given by estimate, and if you decline the service after Rolex looks at the watch, you pay a $280 analysis fee. Rolex also says it may refuse a complete overhaul depending on condition and quote a Restoration offer instead, done in Geneva, with an analysis fee of $2,200 if you turn that down.

## One number to double check

Gear Patrol's October 29 story put the cheapest modern service at $550. When I loaded Rolex's US page on November 3, that same tier read $660. I am going with Rolex's own page, but this is exactly why you should pull up the live list on the day you book, because Rolex can change it. Prices also differ by country. Montenapo Daily, quoting the UK version of the page, listed a starting price of about £550 for modern time only models ([Montenapo Daily](https://montenapodaily.com/en/2026/11/02/trasparenza-rolex-la-maison-pubblica-per-la-prima-volta-i-prezzi-ufficiali-delle-revisioni/)).

## FAQ

**How much does a Rolex service cost?** On Rolex's US page as of November 3, 2026, a complete service runs $660 to $940 for six digit models, $990 to $1,430 for five digit models, and $1,650 to $8,810 for four digit vintage models ([Rolex](https://www.rolex.com/en-us/watch-care-and-service/servicing-prices)).

**Does the price include polishing?** No. Polishing and satin finishing is optional at $170 for modern and classic models and $330 for vintage.

**Can Rolex refuse to service a vintage watch?** Yes. Rolex says it may refuse a complete overhaul depending on condition and offer a Restoration estimate instead.

**Where can I get a Rolex serviced?** Rolex says every Official Rolex Retailer and Rolex affiliate can receive a watch for servicing, and estimates for modern and classic models are free.
