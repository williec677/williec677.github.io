Ice season is officially open. H. Moser took its sportiest Pioneer, bolted on a rotating bezel and a full titanium bracelet, dropped a tourbillon inside and then frosted the dial until it looks like the surface of a frozen lake. It is 100 pieces and it is serious money. Then we swing all the way down the price ladder to a 46 piece COSC chronograph from a tiny Swiss Jura brand that finally made its pilot watch in the size a lot of you have been asking for. Let's get into it.

## H. Moser Pioneer Tourbillon Arctic Fizz

**Announced:** November 18, 2026 ([H. Moser](https://h-moser.com/en/pioneer/pioneer-tourbillon-3805-0500), [Gear Patrol](https://www.gearpatrol.com/watches/moser-pioneer-tourbillon-arctic-fizz/), [Watch Insider](https://watchinsider.com/h-moser-cie-braces-for-winter-with-pioneer-tourbillon-arctic-fizz/), [Hypebeast](https://hypebeast.com/2026/11/h-moser-cie-pioneer-tourbillon-arctic-fizz-limited-edition-release-info))

![H. Moser Pioneer Tourbillon Arctic Fizz in titanium with frosted Arctic Blue fumé dial, tourbillon at six, lumed rotating bezel and titanium bracelet]({{ "/assets/img/watches/h-moser-pioneer-tourbillon-arctic-fizz.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: H. Moser & Cie. (ref. 3805-0500)*

Moser has done the Pioneer with a dive style bezel, a bracelet and a tourbillon before, but only a handful of times, and never with this dial. The new reference 3805-0500 starts with the brand's signature Arctic Blue fumé, bright in the center and darkening toward the edge, then gives it a frosted, grained texture that sparkles like fresh snow. In classic Moser fashion there is no logo on the dial at all, just applied faceted indices, lumed leaf hands and the tourbillon sitting at six o'clock.

Here is what Moser lists on its own product page, with the extra details credited to who reported them:

* **Case:** titanium, 40.0 mm, water resistant to 12 ATM (120 m). Gear Patrol describes a ratcheting unidirectional titanium bezel with every marker lumed. Hypebeast reports 11.4 mm of thickness including the domed sapphire; Moser's page does not list a thickness, so treat that one as reported, not confirmed.
* **Dial:** Arctic Blue fumé with a frosted texture.
* **Movement:** the new automatic Calibre HMC 805 with Moser's double hairspring tourbillon, 72 hours of power reserve. Moser says it replaces the HMC 804. Gear Patrol says it is mechanically the same movement, with more skeletonization, beveling and an anthracite finish. Watch Insider and Hypebeast both note a skeletonized 18 carat red gold rotor, and Hypebeast gives the rate as 21,600 vph.
* **Bracelet:** titanium, with Moser's folding clasp that micro adjusts on the wrist via the push button logo, per Gear Patrol.
* **Limited:** 100 pieces.

**Price:** **$69,000 USD**, the SRP reported by Gear Patrol and Watch Insider. Moser's own site lists **CHF 54,900 excluding VAT**, which is about $65,154 USD at the ECB rate for November 19, 2026. One heads up: Hypebeast prints both "around $65,950 USD" and $69,000 USD in the same article; the $69,000 USD SRP is the figure two outlets agree on, so that is the one we are going with.

**My take:** this is the Pioneer at its absolute best. 40 mm is the right size, titanium keeps it light, 120 m means you can actually swim with a tourbillon, and that frosted fumé is going to photograph like crazy. Is $69,000 USD a lot for a watch you could dive with? Obviously. But at 100 pieces, the real question is whether you can even get on the list. ## Also new: Brellum Pilot 39 Power Reserve Black DLC Chronometer

**Announced:** November 19, 2026 ([Brellum](https://www.brellum.swiss/en/shop/pilot/pilot-39-power-reserve-black-dlc-chronometer.html), [Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/hands-on-introduction-of-the-brellum-pilot-39-power-reserve-black-dlc-chronometer/))

![Brellum Pilot 39 Power Reserve Black DLC Chronometer with black dial, cream lumed Arabic numerals, three subdials, power reserve indicator and black DLC five row bracelet]({{ "/assets/img/watches/brellum-pilot-39-power-reserve-black-dlc.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Brellum (ref. DB.CH.956)*

Brellum is the small Swiss Jura brand run by watchmaker Sébastien Müller, and Fratello notes it caps production at 299 watches a year and sells direct through its own website. Its Pilot chronographs have always been 42 mm and up. Now the brand has finally shrunk the formula, and it went full stealth while doing it: black DLC steel case, black DLC bracelet, black dial and cream lume. The tachymeter bezel from the bigger models is gone, but you keep the 12 hour chronograph, the 46 hour power reserve indicator and a date.

Specs straight from Brellum's product page:

* **Reference:** DB.CH.956, limited to 46 pieces, each rotor individually numbered.
* **Case:** 316L steel with black DLC, 39.50 mm wide, 48 mm lug to lug, 21 mm between the lugs, 100 m water resistance. It is 15.20 mm thick with its domed box sapphire crystals and 10.90 mm without them. Fratello quotes 39 mm and 11.7 mm without the crystals, so the two sources do not match; we are listing the brand's numbers.
* **Crystals:** box shaped sapphire front and back with ARdur and ARunic anti reflective coatings, plus an anti UV layer on the dial side.
* **Dial:** multi level sunshine black dial with recessed counters and Super LumiNova on the indexes and hands.
* **Movement:** Calibre BR-750RM, an automatic chronograph built on the Valjoux 7750 and COSC certified, 25 jewels, 28,800 vph, 46 hours, with Perlage, Côtes de Genève, blued screws and a black DLC Pilot rotor that also gets Super LumiNova.
* **In the box:** both the black DLC steel bracelet and a black calfskin Pilot strap with a deployant buckle.

**Price:** Brellum lists **CHF 3,390 including Swiss VAT**, about **$4,023 USD** at the ECB rate for November 19, 2026. Fratello reports an export price of CHF 3,135 excluding taxes, about $3,721 USD. Brellum has not published a US dollar price. The brand says shipping is insured from Switzerland and included, and it offers interest free installments through Splitit. Worth knowing: Fratello labels its hands on as a preferred position post.

**My take:** a COSC certified 7750 chronograph with a power reserve, a bracelet and a strap in the box and 46 pieces worldwide for around four grand is a strong pitch, and 48 mm lug to lug finally makes it work on smaller wrists. The 15.2 mm height is the trade off of that Valjoux, so know what you are signing up for.
