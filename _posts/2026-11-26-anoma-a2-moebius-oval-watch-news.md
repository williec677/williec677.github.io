Every year a handful of watches make you stop scrolling. Anoma just dropped one of them on Thanksgiving morning. The A2 is the second model from the London design brand behind the triangular A1, and it trades that soft triangle for an oval that literally twists around itself. Hodinkee, Fratello, SJX, Time+Tide and Watchonista all published the same morning, with a full Worn & Wound review shortly after, which tells you how much attention this young brand now commands.

## What is confirmed

**Announced:** November 26, 2026 ([Anoma](https://anomawatches.com/products/a2), [Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/introducing-anoma-a2), [SJX](https://watchesbysjx.com/2026/11/anoma-a2.html), [Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/hands-on-anoma-a2/), [Worn & Wound](https://wornandwound.com/review/review-the-anoma-a2/), [Time+Tide](https://timeandtidewatches.com/anoma-a2-introducing/))

* **The idea:** a Möbius strip, the loop with only one side and one edge. Anoma machines the case from a single block of steel on a five axis CNC machine, and it spirals clockwise to create an asymmetric dial opening, per the brand.
* **The crystal:** a 4 mm thick faceted sapphire crystal that twists the opposite way. Anoma says that from some angles you cannot tell where the case ends and the crystal begins. Time+Tide adds that the crystal is shaped with a diamond tipped tool.
* **The dial:** vertically brushed and finished with three layers of teal blue lacquer, with the logo printed in transparent lacquer at 12 so it appears and disappears in the light.
* **The hands:** inspired by Constantin Brancusi's 1923 sculpture Bird in Space. They are domed, mirror polished and four times thicker than normal hands, and at 10:10 they form an abstract bird, per Anoma.
* **The crown:** hidden on the back of the case at about 1:30, which keeps the side profile completely clean. SJX points out that winding is a little fiddly because the crown is recessed.

## Specs

* **Case:** 316L stainless steel, 41.5 mm long x 35.7 mm wide x 9.8 mm thick, lugless, mirror polished and sandblasted facets. Hodinkee says the brand claims it wears like a 38 mm round watch.
* **Crystal:** 4 mm faceted sapphire. The caseback is solid, so you do not see the movement.
* **Water resistance:** 50 m
* **Movement:** La Joux-Perret D100, hand wound, hours and minutes only, 21,600 vph (3 Hz), 18 jewels, per SJX and Time+Tide. SJX describes it as essentially a clone of the classic Peseux 7001, a smart pick because the train has no center seconds to get in the way of those sculptural hands.
* **Power reserve:** 50 hours according to Time+Tide and SJX. Anoma's own page does not list it, and Hodinkee calls the figure assumed, so treat it as likely rather than official.
* **Strap:** grey grained Italian leather with quick release spring bars and a steel pin buckle that is itself twisted and finished to match the case. Fratello notes the strap slots in underneath the case, like on the A1. Worn & Wound lists the strap width as 16 mm.
* **Limited:** no. The A2 joins Anoma's permanent collection but is made in batches.

## Price and how to order

**£2,400 before taxes (£2,880 with 20% UK VAT), about $3,145 USD as a conversion at the ECB rate of Nov 26, 2026. No official USD price.** US orders ship from within the US with tariffs and duties included, per Anoma. Anoma lists prices only in pounds. For context, Worn & Wound lists **$3,130** and Hodinkee says around $3,150, so the conversion lines up.

**Order window:** Anoma's product page says first run orders are open **November 26 to December 3, 2026**, with **delivery in April 2027**, sold only through anomawatches.com. Time+Tide prints December 6 as the closing date, but we are going with the brand's own page. If you want one from the first batch, do not sleep on it.

**One more to watch:** SJX reports that a one off A2 with a red stromatolite stone dial is in the TimeForArt charity auction at Phillips in New York on December 5.

## Who is Anoma?

Anoma was founded in 2024 by Matteo Violet Vianello, who previously worked at Sotheby's and spent four years at A Collected Man, per Uhren-Nerd. The first watch, the A1, borrowed the shape of a free form 1950s table by Charlotte Perriand, according to Worn & Wound, and it has since come in a string of quick selling limited runs plus permanent editions. The A2 is the brand's first new case shape.

## My take

This is the shaped watch revival at its best. Cartier owns the Baignoire, but nobody else at this price is cutting a one piece faceted case and a counter twisted sapphire crystal and putting a Swiss hand wound movement inside for about $3,145 USD. Hodinkee compares it to the Baignoire and to the current wave of design led indies like Toledano & Chan and Furlan Marri's Disco Volante, and that is the right shelf for it.

The trade offs are real. There are no markers, no seconds hand and no lume, the crown lives on the back, and the official power reserve is not even on the product page yet. This is a watch you buy because the object itself makes you happy, not for the spec sheet. If you want hard horology at a similar price with the same movement maker, look at the La Joux-Perret powered [Lebois & Co Heritage Chronographs]({% post_url 2026-11-26-lebois-heritage-chronograph-frosted-forest-hamilton-jazzmaster-auto-40-casio-gravitymaster-news %}) from this morning, or the Middle East inspired [Gagà Labormatic Arabia]({% post_url 2026-11-25-gaga-labormatic-arabia-akhor-stone-dials-citizen-attesa-ever-since-news %}). And if you love watches where design leads, our tribute to [Alain Silberstein]({% post_url 2026-11-26-alain-silberstein-dies-76-bauhaus-watch-designer-news %}) is a good reminder of how far a bold idea can carry a brand.

On straps: because the A2's strap attaches under the case, do not assume any 16 mm strap will slide in. Check fit with the brand before you buy aftermarket.
