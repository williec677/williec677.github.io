Every price below comes from the brand's or seller's own US website, checked November 19, 2026, in USD. Retailer and Black Friday prices can be lower or higher and change quickly, so check the current price at the link before you buy.

## Orient Bambino Version 7: the dress watch for under $300 USD

If the person on your list wears a button down to work, this is the one. The [Orient Bambino Version 7 in 40.5mm](https://www.orientwatchusa.com/products/ra-ac0033y30b) has a domed crystal, a beige dial with blue hands, and Orient's in house F6724 automatic movement, which hand winds and hacks with a 40 hour power reserve according to Orient. It comes on a brown leather strap with a 21mm lug width. Orient USA lists it at $295 USD.

The one caveat is water resistance. Orient rates it at 30m, so it is a desk and dinner watch, not a swim watch.

## Seiko 5 Sports SRPD55: the first automatic

The [Seiko 5 Sports SRPD55](https://www.seikowatches.com/us-en/products/5sports/srpd55) is the watch I recommend most to people buying their first mechanical watch. Black dial, dive style bezel, a 4R36 automatic with hand winding, 100m of water resistance, and a 22mm lug width that opens up every strap on the market. Seiko's US site lists it at MSRP $350 USD. My full [SRPD buyer's guide](/seiko-5-sports-srpd-buyers-guide/) goes deeper on every colorway.

Shop: {% include aff.html network="amazon" url="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=seiko+5+sports+srpd55" text="Seiko 5 Sports SRPD55 on Amazon" %}

## Tissot PRX 40mm quartz: the integrated bracelet look

If the person on your list loves the integrated bracelet sports watch look, the [Tissot PRX 40mm, reference T137.410.11.041.00](https://www.tissotwatches.com/en-us/t1374101104100.html), is a Swiss made way into it for under $500 USD. Blue dial, steel case and bracelet, Swiss quartz, and 10 bar of water resistance. Tissot's US site lists it at $450 USD. Quartz means no winding and no worrying about whether it stopped in the drawer.

The tradeoff: the bracelet is integrated, so this is not a strap swapping watch. If the person on your list loves changing straps, pick the Seiko. My [Tissot PRX Powermatic 80 buyer's guide](/tissot-prx-powermatic-80-buyers-guide/) covers the automatic version if your budget stretches.

Shop: {% include aff.html network="amazon" url="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=tissot+prx+40mm+quartz+blue+t1374101104100" text="Tissot PRX 40mm quartz on Amazon" %}

## Hemel The Night Passage: the railroad dial automatic for $309 USD

[The Night Passage by Hemel](https://blacklandwatchco.com/products/the-night-passage?ref=MRTICTOCTALK){: rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank"} is a microbrand pick for someone who already owns a Seiko and wants something nobody at the office has. It has a railroad style dial with full lume, a domed sapphire crystal with AR coating, and a Seiko Epson YN55A automatic that hand winds and hacks with a 40 hour power reserve. The 40mm steel case is 13mm thick with a 20mm lug width, a screw down crown, and 200m of water resistance, and it comes on a signed jubilee style bracelet. Blackland Watch Co lists it at $309 USD.

Blackland gave me a coupon code, **MRTICTOCTALK**, to enter at checkout. Check your cart total to see what it takes off before you pay.

## Tsar Bomba Elemental TB8204Q: the bold chronograph under $250 USD

For the person who likes big, loud, sporty watches, the [Tsar Bomba Elemental TB8204Q](https://tsarbomba.com/products/tb8204q?ref=gwjrjgeb){: rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank"} is a tonneau shaped chronograph with a stainless steel case and bezel, a sapphire crystal, and a Seiko quartz movement. Tsar Bomba lists the case at 43 x 50.5mm and 15.5mm thick, so it wears large. It comes on a 26mm silicone strap and is rated to 50m. Tsar Bomba lists every color at $219.99 USD.

The tradeoff: this is a statement watch, not a dress watch, and it is the thickest watch on this list. If the person on your list wants something classic, pick the Bambino.

Shop: <a href="https://tsarbomba.com/products/tb8204q?ref=gwjrjgeb" rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" target="_blank">Elemental TB8204Q at Tsar Bomba</a>

## Thinking vintage instead?

A clean vintage Seiko can make a more personal gift than anything new. Buy from sellers with clear photos of the dial and caseback, and ask about the last service. {% include aff.html network="ebay" url="https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw=vintage+seiko+automatic+watch" text="See current vintage Seiko listings on eBay" %}.

## My pick

For a first watch, the Seiko 5 Sports SRPD55. It is tough, it takes every strap, and at MSRP $350 USD it leaves room in the budget for a NATO strap and a spring bar tool. For someone who dresses up, the Orient Bambino at $295 USD is the classiest watch on this list.
