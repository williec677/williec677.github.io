Big brands love a Saturday off. Small ones use it. At 9 AM Eastern this morning (15:00 CET), Swiss independent Direnzo opened orders on the DRZ 08 Cosmonaire, the first GMT in its history, along with a 40 piece collaboration with British micro brand Isotope. The watch was first shown at the end of October, but today is the day you could actually buy one, and the most wanted version did not last long.

## Direnzo DRZ 08 Cosmonaire GMT

**On sale:** November 21, 2026, 15:00 CET ([Direnzo launch page](https://direnzowatches.com/drz-08-cosmonaire/), [Direnzo DRZ 08 store](https://direnzowatches.com/drz-08/), [Isotope](https://isotopewatches.com/blogs/news/isotope-direnzo-when-two-different-minds-think-alike), [Oracle of Time](https://oracleoftime.com/isotope-direnzo-drz-08-cosmonaire-gmt/), [Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/direnzo-drz-08-cosmonaire-gmt/), [Worn & Wound](https://wornandwound.com/direnzo-introduces-their-first-gmt-the-drz-08-cosmonaire/))

![Isotope x Direnzo DRZ 08 Cosmonaire GMT with grey dial and heat blued hands]({{ "/assets/img/watches/isotope-direnzo-drz-08-cosmonaire-gmt-grey.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Isotope Watches (Isotope x Direnzo DRZ 08 Cosmonaire)*

Direnzo is the work of Sergio Di Renzo, and the brand has built a following on retro futuristic designs that look like props from a 1970s vision of tomorrow. The Cosmonaire keeps that spirit. The rounded case flows into an elliptical bezel, so the profile changes as you tilt your wrist, and the dial is built in layers: a slightly bowl shaped surface, applied hour markers, an engraved ring filled with Super-LumiNova around the edge, and a laser engraved panier (basket weave) pattern under glossy lacquer in the center.

The specs, per Direnzo's launch page and product listings:

* **Case:** 39 mm 316L steel with an HV 800 scratch resistant coating, 46 mm lug to lug, screw down crown, screw down caseback with a sapphire window, 100 m (10 atm) water resistance, about 140 g on the full bracelet.
* **Thickness:** 10.5 mm including the crystal on Direnzo's product pages and on Isotope's page. Direnzo's launch article still says 10 mm, so I am going with the product listings and flagging the gap.
* **Crystal:** domed sapphire with anti reflective coating.
* **Movement:** Swiss Sellita SW330-2 in Elaboré grade with Incabloc shock protection. Direnzo quotes 54 hours of power reserve, while Sellita's own spec for the SW330-2 is 56 hours (Oracle of Time also lists 56). This is a "caller" style GMT: the independently adjustable hand is the 24 hour GMT hand, not the local hour hand. If that difference is new to you, my [affordable Rolex GMT alternatives guide]({% post_url 2026-11-05-affordable-rolex-gmt-alternatives %}) explains true GMT versus caller GMT in plain English.
* **Bracelet:** steel with the same HV 800 coating, screw pins, butterfly clasp and an included half link. A 20 mm leather strap is sold separately for CHF 70, per Direnzo's accessory listing.
* **Warranty:** 24 months.

**Six versions:**

* Blue, green and brown dials with BGW9 lume, plus a brown dial in a gold PVD case and bracelet: CHF 1,200 pre sale, CHF 1,250 retail.
* **Meteorite LE:** heated Muonionalusta meteorite dial with orange lume, 60 pieces, CHF 1,350 pre sale, CHF 1,400 retail.
* **Isotope x Direnzo LE:** grey dial with a vertically brushed center, white lume and heat blued steel hands and cardinal markers, 40 pieces, CHF 1,350 pre sale, CHF 1,400 retail.

**Price in dollars:** Direnzo prices only in Swiss francs. At the ECB reference rate of November 20 (USD 1.0990 and CHF 0.9223 per euro), the standard pre sale price of CHF 1,200 is **about $1,430 USD (CHF 1,200)**, rising to about $1,490 USD (CHF 1,250) at full retail. The two limited editions are **about $1,609 USD (CHF 1,350)** pre sale and about $1,668 USD (CHF 1,400) retail. Direnzo says US orders ship domestically within the United States with no tariffs to pay on delivery. Deliveries are expected in mid January 2027.

## The Isotope edition sold out fast

![Isotope x Direnzo DRZ 08 Cosmonaire caseback showing the Sellita SW330-2 through the sapphire window]({{ "/assets/img/watches/isotope-direnzo-drz-08-cosmonaire-gmt-caseback.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Isotope Watches (Isotope x Direnzo DRZ 08 Cosmonaire caseback)*

The collaboration came out of a conversation between Sergio and Isotope founder José Miranda about three years ago, according to Direnzo. Isotope kept a handful of pieces for UK buyers and the rest went through Direnzo. When I checked both stores at about 12:25 PM ET, the Isotope edition was marked out of stock on Direnzo's site and unavailable on Isotope's, so it sold out in under three and a half hours. The meteorite version was still available, but Direnzo's store showed only 6 left out of 60. The four standard dials were in stock.

## My take

A Swiss made GMT on a steel bracelet for about $1,430 USD is a strong value, and the Cosmonaire brings real design personality to a category full of Rolex lookalikes. If you want a GMT that nobody at the office will mistake for anything else, this is it. Just know what you are buying: a caller GMT is perfect for tracking home time from your desk, but less convenient than a "flyer" GMT when you hop time zones every few days. To see how a flyer GMT works in practice, with its independently jumping local hour hand, read my [step by step guide to setting a GMT Master II]({% post_url 2026-11-18-how-to-set-rolex-gmt-master-ii %}).
