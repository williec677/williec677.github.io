## Perpetual calendars

A regular date window assumes every month has 31 days, so you correct it five times a year, after February, April, June, September, and November. A perpetual calendar knows which months are short, and it knows leap years. On February 28 it can roll to March 1, or to February 29 in a leap year, on its own.

### Citizen PCAT CB5916-59L

Official US list price USD 775, official promotional price USD 620, both shown separately on [Citizen's US product page](https://www.citizenwatch.com/us/en/product/CB5916-59L) when it was opened on November 18, 2026.

This is a solar powered quartz watch. The calendar is handled by electronics. The official Citizen E660 instruction manual says you will not need to correct the date for short months or leap years until February 28, 2100. After a full charge, the official running period is about 10 months when the power save function works for 4 hours a day in normal use, or about 3 years if power save runs all day. When reception works, the watch can also receive time signals and adjust the time and calendar. If the calendar is wrong, the official manual walks you through setting it. I am not repeating those button by button steps here, because they are already written clearly in the [official E660 manual](https://citizenwatch-global.com/support/pdf/e660/e.pdf).

Why 2100? The Gregorian calendar skips the leap day in years that are divisible by 100 but not by 400, so 2100 has no February 29.

### Frederique Constant Classic Perpetual Calendar FC-776S3H6

Official US price USD 12,995, official silver tone dial, on [Frederique Constant's US product page](https://us.frederiqueconstant.com/product/FC-776S3H6.html), opened November 18, 2026. Official functions are hours, minutes, date, day, month, leap year, and moonphase. Official power reserve is 72 hours, about three days off the wrist once the watch is fully wound.

Official brand materials say that in daily use you only set the hours and minutes with the crown. The calendar functions are set with dedicated pushers recessed in the case. As long as the watch keeps running, the calendar takes care of itself. If it does stop, you do not start over. You bring the calendar back up to date with those recessed pushers. After a stop longer than the official 72 hour reserve, the calendar should be set again.

I am not applying the Citizen 2100 rule to this watch. Frederique Constant has not published a 2100 statement for the FC-776. Most mechanical perpetual calendars that simply count every fourth year as a leap year will need a one day correction in 2100. That is a general calendar fact, not a claim about this specific movement.

**Takeaway:** More complex does not automatically mean more practical every day. The Citizen may be the easier everyday choice. The Frederique Constant solves the same problem with gears instead of a circuit. That is a different kind of achievement, not a better date display.

## Sun and moon versus moonphase

### Orient Sun and Moon RA-AK0007S30B

Official current selling price USD 450, listed regular price USD 705, on [Orient's official US page](https://www.orientwatchusa.com/products/ra-ak0007s30b), opened November 18, 2026. Official functions include a 24 hour sun and moon display, a day of the week display, and a date. Over one day the display turns from sun to moon and back, so it shows roughly whether it is day or night. The official page does not describe this as a moonphase.

### Tissot Carson Premium T1224231103300

Official US price USD 495, on [Tissot's official US page](https://www.tissotwatches.com/en-us/T1224231103300.html), opened November 18, 2026. Official function: moonphase. It follows the moon from new, to full, and back again, over about 29 and a half days.

**Takeaway:** A moon on the dial is not necessarily a moonphase. If you want the lunar month, the Carson is the one that does that job. If you want a day and night display, the Orient does exactly what it was designed to do.

## GMT types

Quick question first. When you fly from New York to London, which hand would you rather move: the one that stays on home time, or the one that shows local time? The official crown procedures decide the answer. Collectors often call these caller GMT and flyer GMT. Both are real GMT watches.

Most of the year, London is five hours ahead of New York. The United States and the United Kingdom change their clocks on different dates, so for a few weeks in spring, and about a week in the fall, the gap is only four hours. In 2026 that shorter gap runs from the second Sunday in March to the last Sunday in March, and from October 25 to November 1. The gap does not grow to six hours.

### Seiko 5 Sports Field GMT SSK023

Official US price USD 450, official caliber 4R34, on [Seiko's official US page](https://www.seikowatches.com/us-en/products/5sports/ssk023), opened November 18, 2026. The hand you move on its own is the 24 hour hand. That is useful if you stay home and keep track of another city's time.

Official [Seiko 4R34 instructions](https://www.seikowatches.com/instructions/html/SEIKO_4R34_EN/index):

- First click of the crown: turn the crown counterclockwise to set the date, or clockwise to set the 24 hour hand independently.
- Second click: set the time with the hour and minute hands.

Seiko describes two uses for that 24 hour hand: checking AM and PM, or indicating the time of a second region.

### Longines Spirit Zulu Time L3.802.4.53.6

Official US price USD 3,550 for the 39 mm steel watch with the black matt dial, on [Longines' official US page](https://www.longines.com/en-us/p/watch-longines-spirit-zulu-time-l3-802-4-53-6), opened November 18, 2026. The hand you move on its own is the local hour hand. It jumps one hour at a time, and home time and the minutes stay put. That is useful when you are the one changing cities.

Official [Longines L844 instruction manual](https://api.ecom.longines.com/media/manual/pdf/Mode_emploi_L844-EN_260826.pdf):

- Crown position 2: the local hour hand jumps in one hour steps, without affecting the minute, the seconds, or the second time zone display. The date changes when that hour hand passes midnight. After a month shorter than 31 days, the date still needs a correction.
- Crown position 3: setting the time also turns the second time zone hand.

**Takeaway:** The hand that moves on its own decides which watch suits the way you travel.

## Chronographs

A chronograph is a stopwatch built into a watch. The two examples in the video do the same basic job, start, stop, and reset, with very different engineering.

### Rolex Cosmograph Daytona 126500LN

Official US price USD 16,900 for the Oystersteel watch, on [Rolex's official US page](https://www.rolex.com/en-us/watches/cosmograph-daytona/m126500ln-0001), opened November 18, 2026. On this Daytona you unscrew the pushers first. The top pusher starts and stops the center seconds hand. The bottom pusher resets the counters to zero. The small dial at 3 counts up to 30 minutes, the one at 9 counts up to 12 hours, and the one at 6 is the running small seconds.

Rolex says the Daytona's vertical clutch gives an instant, precise start. A column wheel, a small wheel with towers on top, turns one step with every push and tells the clutch when to move. The scale on the bezel is a tachymeter. Start the chronograph at one mile marker and stop it at the next. The seconds hand then points to your average speed. Forty five seconds means 80 miles per hour.

### Tissot PRX Automatic Chronograph T1374271104101

Official US price USD 2,150, official movement name Valjoux A05.H31, on [Tissot's official US page](https://www.tissotwatches.com/en-us/T1374271104101.html), opened November 18, 2026. It starts, stops, and resets the same way. The Valjoux movement uses a cam and levers instead of a column wheel. It does the same job.

### Tissot PRX quartz chronograph T1374171105100

Official US price USD 525, on [Tissot's official US page](https://www.tissotwatches.com/en-us/T1374171105100.html), opened November 18, 2026. It appears in the video's five item rundown and in the next video teaser. Quartz usually keeps tighter time day to day. Collectors still love a mechanical chronograph because they are not only buying a stopwatch. They are buying a movement they can see, hear, and feel.

**Takeaway:** The same function can be built in completely different ways.

## World time

The video uses the same three cities, New York, London, and Tokyo, on two very different watches.

### Casio AE-1200WH-1AV

The official Casio US list price is USD 40, checked November 19, 2026. Casio's own US product page was not readable from our tools that day, so that figure is the official list price as shown by a major US retailer. The official product page is [casio.com for AE-1200WH-1AV](https://www.casio.com/us/watches/casio/product.AE-1200WH-1AV/). Setting steps below come from the [official Casio Operation Guide 3198 and 3299](https://www.casio.com/content/dam/casio/global/support/manuals/watches/pdf/31/3198/qw3198_EN.pdf), downloaded November 16, 2026.

Official World Time shows the current time in 48 cities, 31 time zones, and UTC. Official city codes include NYC for New York, offset minus 5 hours; LON for London, offset 0; and TYO for Tokyo, offset plus 9 hours. Casio notes that the city table is based on data as of December 2010, and that summer time rules are determined by each country.

Official World Time setting, paraphrased from that guide:

- Enter World Time Mode by pressing C.
- Press D to scroll east through city codes, one city at a time.
- You can toggle Daylight Saving Time or standard time for the city currently on the display. That change affects only the displayed city, not the others. Do not expect to toggle it while UTC is selected.
- In those odd weeks when New York and London are only four hours apart, you would turn summer time on for New York and leave London on standard time. Tokyo does not use daylight saving, so you leave it alone.

The same guide also describes Multi Time: one Home City Time and three Local Times. For those local times, you can change the city code and the DST setting.

### Frederique Constant Classic Worldtimer FC-719NN3H6B

Official US price USD 6,195, official Dial Color Blue, on [Frederique Constant's US product page](https://us.frederiqueconstant.com/product/FC-719NN3H6B.html), opened November 18, 2026. Official text describes navy blue oceans and a city disc, a date free dial, a 40 mm case, and a 72 hour reserve. All functions are adjustable with the single crown. Official materials do not describe automatic daylight saving, so do not expect the city disc to jump when the clocks change. New York, London, and Tokyo can all be read at the same moment.

**Takeaway:** Checking time zones one at a time, and seeing them all at once, are two different experiences.

## What I would take from the comparisons

If you remember one thing, make it this. The name of a complication does not tell you the whole story. Sometimes you are paying for function. Sometimes you are paying for craft. Know which one you want before you buy.
