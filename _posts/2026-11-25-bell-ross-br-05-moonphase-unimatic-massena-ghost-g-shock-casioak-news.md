The day before Thanksgiving is usually quiet, but Bell & Ross clearly did not get the memo. The BR-05 finally gets a moon, Unimatic and Massena LAB drop another tight little collab, and G-Shock leans even harder into the CasiOak nickname. Here's what matters.

## Bell & Ross BR-05 Moonphase

**Announced:** November 25, 2026 ([Bell & Ross US](https://www.bellross.com/en-us/products/br-05-moonphase-steel-bracelet), [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/bell-ross-br-05-moonphase-aventurine-introducing-specs-price/), [uhren-nerd](https://www.uhren-nerd.de/bell-ross-br-05-moonphase/))

The BR-05 has been Bell & Ross's integrated bracelet sports watch since 2019, and it has already branched into skeletons, a GMT, a chronograph and the chunkier BR-X5. What it never had was a moonphase. Bell & Ross says this is the first time the Maison has built a complication dedicated to the phases of the Moon, and it did not take the easy route.

Most moonphases show the Moon through a fan shaped cutout, with two humps of dial hiding parts of a rotating disc. Here there is one large round window at 6 o'clock, and the Moon itself never moves. A grey PVD coated sapphire plate, laser engraved with the seas and craters, sits on top. Underneath, a rotating photoluminescent disc slides the shadow across, so the full lunar surface is always visible and only the light changes through the eight phases. That disc glows in the dark, which means the Moon is the brightest thing on the dial at night.

![Bell & Ross BR-05 Moonphase dial close up showing the black aventurine and engraved moon aperture]({{ "/assets/img/watches/bell-ross-br-05-moonphase-dial.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Bell & Ross via Monochrome*

The rest of the dial stays out of the way. Black aventurine glass gives you the starry sky, there is no seconds hand and no date, and the rhodium plated applied indices and hands are not lumed, per Monochrome. On the back, a solid caseback carries an embossed full moon.

### Specs

* **Reference:** BR05A-MP-ST/SST (steel bracelet) and BR05A-MP-ST/SRB (rubber strap), per Bell & Ross US
* **Case:** stainless steel, 40 mm wide, 11.5 mm thick, satin brushed and polished, four screw bezel, screw down crown with guards
* **Dial:** black aventurine, round moonphase aperture at 6 o'clock with engraved sapphire Moon and luminous rotating phase disc
* **Crystal:** sapphire with anti reflective coating
* **Water resistance:** 100 m
* **Movement:** BR-CAL.330 automatic, based on the La Joux-Perret G101 at 4 Hz per Monochrome; hours, minutes and moonphase
* **Power reserve:** about 60 hours per Monochrome and Bell & Ross's own product story, though the spec list on the same Bell & Ross page says about 65 hours. We will update if the brand clarifies.
* **Bracelet or strap:** integrated steel H link bracelet with folding clasp, or ribbed black rubber with steel folding clasp
* **Edition:** permanent collection

![Bell & Ross BR-05 Moonphase solid steel caseback with embossed full moon]({{ "/assets/img/watches/bell-ross-br-05-moonphase-caseback.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Bell & Ross via Monochrome*

**Price and availability:** **$5,800 USD** on rubber and **$6,300 USD** on the steel bracelet on Bell & Ross's US site, available now. In Europe it is EUR 5,500 and EUR 5,990, per Monochrome.

**My take:** this is the right way to do a moonphase on a sports watch. A big, honest Moon that is always fully visible looks modern instead of borrowed from a dress watch, and dropping the date keeps the dial calm. The non lumed hands are a strange call on a watch that glows at 6 o'clock, so check the night legibility in person. Compared with the [MoonSwatch Silver Snowflake]({% post_url 2026-11-25-moonswatch-mission-to-earthphase-silver-snowflake-news %}) that dropped this morning, this is the grown up, steel bracelet way into a moon dial, and at $6,300 USD it lands in the middle of the integrated sports watch fight.

## Unimatic x Massena LAB Ghost Collection

**Announced:** November 25, 2026 ([Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/unimatic-x-massena-lab-ghost-collection/), [Unimatic UT5-G-DS](https://www.unimatic.it/products/uwk-ut5-g-ds), [Unimatic UT5-G-NV](https://www.unimatic.it/products/uwk-ut5-g-nv))

![Unimatic x Massena LAB Ghost Modello Cinque UT5-G-NV navy and UT5-G-DS desert sand on matching suede straps]({{ "/assets/img/watches/unimatic-massena-lab-ghost-ut5-g.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Unimatic via Fratello*

Unimatic and Massena LAB have been collaborating since the Modello Uno U1-ML6 in 2020, and the new Ghost pair is all about camouflage by subtraction. Instead of a pattern, everything goes one color so the watch simply melts into its surroundings. Fratello says the idea traces back to a private 2023 friends and family piece, the UT4-C Testing Unit.

* **UT5-G-DS:** steel case in Cerakote H-199 Desert Sand with a matching sand dial and golden sand text, minute track, indices and hands.
* **UT5-G-NV:** steel case in Cerakote H-127 Kel-Tec navy with a textured navy dial and the same golden sand details, which gives a bit more contrast.

### Specs

* **Case:** 316L stainless steel with Cerakote finish, 36 mm, 43.7 mm lug to lug, 13.5 mm thick, fixed monoblock bezel, 8 mm screw down crown
* **Crystal:** double domed sapphire, inner anti reflective coating
* **Water resistance:** 300 m
* **Movement:** Seiko VH31A quartz with sweeping seconds, plus or minus 15 seconds per month, about two years of battery life, inside Unimatic's 360 degree shock protection system
* **Lume:** Super-LumiNova Old Radium on indices and hands
* **Strap:** tone matched 22 mm quick release suede with Cerakote pin buckle
* **Testing:** MIL-STD-810 per Unimatic
* **Edition:** 200 individually numbered pieces of each

**Price and availability:** **$785 USD** (EUR 675) per Fratello. Both references went live on Unimatic's store this morning, and Fratello says they will also sell through Massena LAB and the Unimatic flagship in Milan. Unimatic's own store showed EUR 710 when we checked, so expect the final number to depend on where you buy and local tax.

**My take:** a 36 mm, 300 m quartz tool watch with a sweep seconds hand and a real design idea for under $800 USD is a fun buy, and 200 pieces per color will not hang around long once collab fans find it. The desert sand one is the hero for me.

## G-Shock GBM-2100LXB-1A2 Luxe Black CasiOak

**US availability:** reported November 25, 2026 ([Gear Patrol](https://www.gearpatrol.com/watches/g-shock-gbm-2100lxb-1a2-casioak-united-states-availability/), [Gizmochina](https://www.gizmochina.com/2026/11/25/casio-g-shock-gbm2100lxb-1a2-launched-specs-price/), [uhren-nerd](https://www.uhren-nerd.de/en/casio-g-shock-gbm-2100lxb-1a2/))

![G-Shock GBM-2100LXB-1A2 with black IP steel bezel, checkered black dial and blue accents]({{ "/assets/img/watches/g-shock-gbm-2100lxb-1a2.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Casio via Gear Patrol*

The 2100 got the CasiOak nickname for its octagonal bezel, and the newest Luxe Black version goes straight for the dial too. The GBM-2100LXB-1A2 gets a raised grid pattern that Gear Patrol calls a nod to the Royal Oak's Tapisserie, matched by a square dot texture on the strap. Casio adds a black IP steel bezel, gunmetal indices, a blue treated outer edge on the crystal and a blue mode hand.

* **Case:** bio based resin with black IP stainless steel bezel, 49.3 x 44.4 x 11.9 mm, 72 g
* **Crystal:** mineral glass
* **Water resistance:** 200 m, shock resistant
* **Power:** Tough Solar, about 7 months on a charge in normal use or 18 months in darkness with power saving
* **Connectivity:** Bluetooth Smartphone Link with the CASIO WATCHES app (auto time, about 300 world time cities, reminders, phone finder)
* **Functions:** world time in 38 zones, 1/100 second stopwatch, 60 minute timer, 5 alarms, double LED light, hand shift, full auto calendar to 2099

**Price:** **$310 USD**, now on G-Shock's US site per Gear Patrol, which notes it is $50 more than the previous Luxe Black edition. Germany lists it at EUR 289, per uhren-nerd.

**My take:** $310 USD for a solar, Bluetooth CasiOak with a steel bezel and the best dial texture the 2100 has had is a strong stocking stuffer for the enthusiast in your life.
