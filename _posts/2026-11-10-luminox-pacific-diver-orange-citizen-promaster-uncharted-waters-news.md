Orange and dive watches go together like steel and sapphire, and tonight two brands that live on opposite ends of the budget scale both leaned into it. **Luminox** officially announced the **Pacific Diver Automatic Limited Edition XS.3109.N** today, a 400 piece run with a burnt orange ceramic bezel. And **Citizen** has opened US sales of its **Promaster Dive Uncharted Waters** trio, solar powered divers built with sport fishing champion Peter Miller, starting at $425. Every spec and price below comes from the brands' own product pages and press release, checked on November 10, 2026, with outside reporting linked where I used it.

## Luminox Pacific Diver Automatic Limited Edition XS.3109.N

Luminox is a quartz brand to most people, the watch on the wrist of soldiers and rescue crews with those glowing tritium tubes. The Pacific Diver Automatic is its mechanical flagship diver, and this limited edition changes one big thing: the bezel insert is now burnt orange ceramic instead of the usual black, blue or green. Luminox Europe posted the [official announcement](https://eu.luminox.com/blogs/news/luminox-launcht-die-neue-pacific-diver-automatic-limited-edition) on November 10, though the [US product page](https://luminox.com/products/pacific-diver-automatic-3100-series-limited-edition) went up a few days earlier and [The Gadgeteer](https://the-gadgeteer.com/2026/11/06/luminox-pacific-diver-automatic-limited-edition-orange-bezel/) spotted it on November 6.

| Case | 42 mm 316L stainless steel, about 13 mm thick (The Gadgeteer), 121 g listed weight (Luminox Canada) |

| Price | **$1,745** in the US, £1,489 in the UK, €1,395 in Europe, CAD 2,550 in Canada |

The US price is the retail price on luminox.com, where it showed as in stock when I checked. This is a brand new limited edition, so there are no secondary market sales to compare yet.

### Why it works

The orange is restrained in the best way. According to The Gadgeteer's hands on, the dial stays black, and the only other orange touches are the AUTOMATIC print above 6 and the tip of the seconds hand. From across a room it reads as a black diver, up close it pops. Ceramic also matters here: an aluminum insert can fade and scratch, while ceramic keeps that color looking new for years.

The real hook is still the light. Those self powered LLT tubes glow all night without charging up under a lamp, and that is something most automatic divers at this price simply do not offer. Pair that with a Sellita SW200-1, a movement any competent watchmaker can service, and you have a tool watch you can actually live with.

### My take

Be honest with yourself about what the money buys. The Gadgeteer compares it with the regular blue Pacific Diver Automatic XS.3104 and notes the movement, case and 200 m rating are the same, so the premium is the orange ceramic and a numbered piece out of 400. If orange is your color and you want a diver that glows like no other, it is a fun buy before the run sells through. If you just want a Luminox automatic, the standard model does the same job.

## Citizen Promaster Dive Uncharted Waters: now in the US

The second story is pure value. Citizen's Promaster Dive Uncharted Waters collection is a collaboration with sport fisherman Peter Miller, and [Gizmochina](https://www.gizmochina.com/2026/11/10/promaster-dive-uncharted-waters-watches-launched-specs-price/) reports the three watches launched in Canada last month before going on sale through Citizen's US site this week. All three are Eco-Drive, meaning light of any kind charges them and there is no battery to swap, and all three are ISO rated for scuba diving at 200 m with sapphire crystals and a date.

| Reference | Case | Dial and details | Strap | US price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [BN0251-04L](https://www.citizenwatch.com/us/en/product/BN0251-04L.html) | 44 mm steel | Light blue dial | Navy polyurethane strap, buckle | **$425** |
| [BN0276-54L](https://www.citizenwatch.com/us/en/product/BN0276-54L.html) | 40.6 mm steel | Blue dial, orange seconds hand | Steel bracelet, push button fold over clasp | **$450** |
| [BN0270-51E](https://www.citizenwatch.com/us/en/product/BN0270-51E.html) | 40.6 mm steel | Black dial, orange unidirectional bezel | Steel bracelet, push button fold over clasp | **$450** |

Prices are Citizen's US retail on citizenwatch.com. One honest note: Citizen's own pages are a little messy. The spec tables list 40.6 mm cases for the two bracelet models while the marketing copy says 41 mm, and the pages name two different caliber numbers, so I am going with the spec table size and not naming a caliber until Citizen cleans that up.

### My take

The BN0270-51E is the one I would grab. Black dial, orange bezel, steel bracelet, sapphire, solar power and a real ISO dive rating for $450 is hard to argue with, and the 40.6 mm size will fit far more wrists than the 44 mm strap model. It is the kind of beater you throw on for a boat day and never think about again. If you want the classic big Citizen diver instead, the Promaster BN0150 is still around, and I have a [strap guide for the Citizen Promaster BN0150](https://mrtictoctalk.com/strap-finder/citizen-promaster-bn0150/) in my Watch Strap Finder.
