Cabot Watch Company just gave its most storied Royal Navy diver a civilian refresh. The CWC Navy Eighty CD-1 keeps the 1980 Clearance Diver case shape and dial language, then adds a ceramic bezel, applied luminous markers, and a Sellita SW200-1 Top Grade automatic. Five dial and lume combinations all land at £2,500. I checked every size, price, and availability note below against [CWC](https://www.cwcwatch.com/products/cwc-navy-eighty-cd1-automatic-divers-watch) and [Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/cwc-navy-eighty-automatic) on November 6, 2026.

## Why the Navy Eighty matters

In 1980, CWC won the contract to supply the Royal Navy's new automatic divers' watch, stepping into a role previously associated with the Rolex Submariner for Clearance Divers ([CWC](https://www.cwcwatch.com/products/cwc-navy-eighty-cd1-automatic-divers-watch)). Those early issued pieces are among the most chased CWC unit watches. The Navy Eighty CD-1 is not a museum replica. It is a modern CWC built around that silhouette for civilians who want the heritage without hunting a battered issued example.

Hodinkee's Introducing piece flags the updates that matter most: applied markers with metal surrounds, a ceramic bezel insert (Hodinkee says this is the first ceramic bezel on a CWC Royal Navy Diver), diamond tipped luminous seconds hand closer to 1980 originals, and the old school non serif CWC logo ([Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/cwc-navy-eighty-automatic)). Dial text at 6 o'clock and X1 Super-LumiNova in Light Vintage, Vintage, and BGW9 shades finish the separation from the standard Royal Navy Diver still in the catalog.

## Specs from the brand

Per [CWC's product page](https://www.cwcwatch.com/products/cwc-navy-eighty-cd1-automatic-divers-watch), the polished stainless steel 1980 pattern case measures 41mm excluding the crown and 45mm including it, 47mm lug to lug, 20mm lug width, and 12.7mm from the top of the crystal to the base of the caseback. Bezel diameter is 40mm. Crystal diameter is 31mm. Weight without strap is 79g. Water resistance is 30 atmospheres (300m). The crystal is sapphire. Crown and caseback are screw down. Strap bars are fixed for pass through straps. Swiss made.

The bezel is a one way luminous time elapse insert with 60 clicks. Hodinkee confirms the insert is ceramic ([Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/cwc-navy-eighty-automatic)). Movement is the Sellita SW200-1 Top Grade automatic, CWC's practical successor to the ETA movements that powered original mechanical Royal Navy Divers. Sword hands sit over applied luminous markers.

## Five dial and lume configs

All variants use X1 Super-LumiNova. Dial and hand options from the brand:

**Blue dial**
* Vintage lume with white hands
* BGW9 lume with white hands
* BGW9 lume with silver hands

**Black dial**
* Light Vintage lume with silver hands
* Light Vintage lume with white hands

Vintage colored lume reads warmer in daylight. BGW9 stays white in day and glows blue at night ([CWC](https://www.cwcwatch.com/products/cwc-navy-eighty-cd1-automatic-divers-watch)). Blue watches ship on a 20mm navy stretch strap. Black watches ship on a black and tan single pass ribbed strap. Both also include a standard nylon military strap.

## Price and availability

**Price:** £2,500 including VAT, or £2,083.33 without VAT. Hodinkee converts the ex VAT figure to about $2,700 ([Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/cwc-navy-eighty-automatic)). All five configurations share that price.

**Edition status:** Not a numbered limited edition. Produced in small batches, so waiting is possible if a configuration sells through ([Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/cwc-navy-eighty-automatic)).

**Availability note:** As of this writing on November 6, 2026, the [CWC product page](https://www.cwcwatch.com/products/cwc-navy-eighty-cd1-automatic-divers-watch) shows Unavailable. Treat that as a live stock flag on a small batch release, not as discontinued. Confirm current status on the brand site before you assume a colorway is open.
