Thanksgiving eve turned into a little Panerai story. Panerai dropped a 30 piece Luna Rossa watch that comes with a seat at the America's Cup, and on the same day Bonhams put up the watch Britain built in WWII because it could not get Panerais of its own. In between, Jaeger-LeCoultre quietly filled a gap in the Reverso lineup. Here's what matters.

## Panerai Luminor Luna Rossa GMT Experience Edition PAM01464

**Announced:** November 25, 2026 ([Haute Time](https://www.hautetime.com/panerai-luminor-luna-rossa-gmt-experience-edition-2026/), [Oracle of Time](https://oracleoftime.com/panerai-introduce-luminor-luna-rossa-gmt-experience-edition/))

Panerai has sponsored the Italian sailing team Luna Rossa since 2019, and the 38th America's Cup is headed to Naples in 2027, the first time the race will be sailed in Italy. Earlier this year Panerai released a Luna Rossa chronograph and a 40 mm GMT for the campaign. This one is different: it is the first open worked Luminor in the Luna Rossa collection, and it is an Experience Edition, which means the watch comes bundled with a trip.

Each of the 30 owners gets a place at a five day Luna Rossa Experience in Naples, June 16 to 20, 2027. Per Haute Time, that covers access to the team base and dock out operations, following the racing with the crew, time on the water around Naples, visits to Capri and the submerged Roman city of Baia, and time aboard Eilean, the classic ketch Panerai owns.

![Panerai Luminor Luna Rossa GMT Experience Edition PAM01464 front view showing the skeletonized grid dial and red accented rubber strap]({{ "/assets/img/watches/panerai-luminor-luna-rossa-gmt-experience-pam01464-front.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Panerai via Haute Time*

The dial is a black grid that floats the time display over the movement. Haute Time says the grid nods to the anti submarine nets once stretched under naval vessels, which Italian Navy frogmen had to cut through. You get central hours and minutes, a central 12 hour GMT hand, small seconds at 9 with a 24 hour AM/PM indication, and Panerai's patented polarized date at 3, where only the current date shows and the rest of the disc stays clear. The remaining power reserve is shown on a rotating sapphire disc on the back.

The GMT is a proper traveler's GMT: local time jumps forward or back in one hour steps without stopping the watch, and the date follows local time in both directions. A seconds reset in the second crown position zeros the small seconds, which is handy for synchronizing to a race start.

### Specs

* **Reference:** PAM01464
* **Case:** Carbotech (carbon fiber compressed with PEEK) case, bezel and crown protecting bridge, 44 mm, titanium inner container, Grade 5 titanium screwed caseback with sapphire window
* **Crystal:** sapphire, thicker front and back crystals per Haute Time
* **Water resistance:** 100 m (10 bar)
* **Movement:** Panerai P.4001/S automatic, skeletonized, two barrels in series, off centered bidirectional tungsten micro rotor, 4 Hz, 341 components, 31 jewels, 7.36 mm thick
* **Power reserve:** 3 days (72 hours)
* **Functions:** hours, minutes, small seconds with seconds reset, GMT with 24 hour indication, polarized date, power reserve on the back
* **Strap:** Luna Rossa hook and loop strap, plus a black rubber strap with PAM Click Release and a black DLC titanium trapezoidal buckle, 24 mm at the lugs tapering to 22 mm, in a mahogany box with a screwdriver
* **Edition:** 30 pieces, each with a five day Luna Rossa Experience in Naples, June 16 to 20, 2027

**Price and availability:** price on request, per Oracle of Time and Haute Time. Panerai has not published a number, so we will not guess one.

**My take:** I like that the travel GMT and the open movement got the 100 m rating to go with them, which is the hard part with a sapphire backed skeleton. The watch itself is a niche piece, but the real product here is a five day Luna Rossa Experience in Naples, June 16 to 20, 2027. ## Jaeger-LeCoultre Reverso Classic Monoface Small Seconds in Pink Gold

**Announced:** November 25, 2026 ([Oracle of Time](https://oracleoftime.com/jaeger-lecoultre-reverso-classic-monoface-small-seconds-pink-gold/), [CJ Charles, authorized dealer listing](https://www.cjcharles.com/products/reverso-classic-monoface-small-seconds-q3862520))

![Jaeger-LeCoultre Reverso Classic Monoface Small Seconds Q3862520 in pink gold with silvered grey dial and black strap]({{ "/assets/img/watches/jaeger-lecoultre-reverso-classic-monoface-small-seconds-pink-gold-q3862520.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Jaeger-LeCoultre via Oracle of Time*

The Reverso Classic Monoface Small Seconds has been a steel watch in two sizes, 45.6 x 27.4 mm and 40.1 x 24.4 mm. Oracle of Time reports the new Q3862520 is the first precious metal version, in 18k pink gold at the smaller size, and that it went live on Jaeger-LeCoultre's online boutique over the weekend before this morning's press announcement. One note: a US authorized dealer, CJ Charles, has had the reference listed since October 23, so it is more of an official confirmation than a surprise.

The dial matches the steel watch: silvered grey with a vertical satin brushed outer track, black Arabic numerals, a textured center and a rectangular small seconds at 6. The JL logo is pink gold to match the case, and the sword hands and baton seconds hand are blued. The case still flips over, the trick Jaeger-LeCoultre invented for polo players in 1931.

### Specs

* **Reference:** Q3862520
* **Case:** 18k pink gold, 40.1 x 24.4 mm, 7.56 mm thick, reversible
* **Dial:** silvered grey, vertical satin brushed and textured, black transferred numerals, small seconds at 6
* **Water resistance:** 30 m
* **Movement:** Jaeger-LeCoultre calibre 822 manual wind, 42 hour power reserve; hours, minutes, small seconds
* **Strap:** black alligator per Oracle of Time

**Price:** **$18,400 USD**, US retail price per Jaeger-LeCoultre's launch figures, as reported by Monochrome. In the UK it is £16,500 (about $21,550 USD at the November 25 ECB rate), per Oracle of Time.

**My take:** this is gap filling, not a revolution, and that is fine. A slim, hand wound, pink gold Reverso at 40 mm tall is about as classic as dress watches get. If you are following the Bond watch chatter, Jaeger-LeCoultre is one of the two names in the frame, as we laid out in our [Robert Pattinson Bond watch story]({% post_url 2026-11-16-robert-pattinson-james-bond-omega-jaeger-lecoultre-g-shock-mudman-gdgb100-news %}).

## Bonhams Offers HS C13, the WWII Longines Royal Navy "British Panerai"

**Auction:** London Fine Watches, Bonhams New Bond Street, Thursday December 10, 2026 at 14:00 GMT (9 AM ET), Lot 7 ([Bonhams lot](https://www.bonhams.com/auction/31985/lot/7/longines-an-extremely-rare-and-large-silver-manual-wind-wwii-british-military-divers-wristwatch-made-for-the-royal-navy-circa-1943/), [Bonhams press release](https://www.bonhams.com/press_release/41961/), [Oracle of Time](https://oracleoftime.com/wwii-longines-british-royal-navy-dive-watch/))

![WWII Longines Royal Navy HS C13 diver's watch with 51 mm silver case, black radium dial and chained crown cap]({{ "/assets/img/watches/longines-royal-navy-hs-c13-bonhams.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Bonhams via Oracle of Time*

Here is the link to the Panerai story above. In December 1941 Italian frogmen riding manned torpedoes crippled HMS Valiant and HMS Queen Elizabeth in Alexandria harbour, and Oracle of Time notes they almost certainly wore Panerais to time the attack. Britain built its own manned torpedo, the Chariot, and its crews needed a watertight watch. Apart from Panerai, nobody made one, so the Admiralty's Hydrographic department had one built: Longines supplied the movements and A.T. Oliver of Clerkenwell made the cases.

Bonhams calls the HS C Britain's first purpose built military diving watch, developed in response to Panerai's Radiomir, and says it predates the Rolex Submariner by a decade. The watch on offer is HS C13, engraved on the caseback, which Oracle of Time describes as the second earliest recorded example.

![Caseback of the Longines Royal Navy HS C13 engraved with the broad arrow and HS C13 marking]({{ "/assets/img/watches/longines-royal-navy-hs-c13-bonhams-caseback.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Bonhams via Oracle of Time*

### What is documented

* **Case:** sterling silver, 51 mm across and 17 mm tall per Oracle of Time, notched bezel and caseback tightened with a wrench, screw down cap over the crown chained to the case
* **Dial:** matte black with radium numerals at 12, 3, 6 and 9, original per Bonhams
* **Movement:** Longines calibre 12.68N, 16 jewels, manual wind; Oracle of Time notes this example has no seconds hand
* **Condition:** original silver case, matching numbered components and original radium dial, per Bonhams
* **Rarity:** Bonhams says fewer than 100 were believed made and fewer than 10 survive; Oracle of Time puts the known count at eight before HS C13 surfaced. Both point the same way, just phrased differently.
* **History:** Bonhams says it was issued to Chariot crews and "may also have seen service" in preparations for the D Day landings. That is the auction house's wording, not a confirmed service record.
* **Estimate:** £30,000 to £50,000, about **$39,200 to $65,300 USD** at the November 25 ECB rate, excluding the buyer's premium
* **Last comparable:** Oracle of Time says the last complete, original HS C at auction was HS C15 at Antiquorum in 2007, which sold for CHF 37,760

Public viewing at New Bond Street runs December 6 (12:00 to 16:00) and December 7 to 9 (10:00 to 17:00), per Oracle of Time.

The same sale is led by a gem set Rolex Daytona "SACO" ref. 116578 from 2019 estimated at £130,000 to £230,000 (about $169,800 to $300,400 USD), with a Patek Philippe Nautilus 3700/1A "Jumbo" circa 1980 and an Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Quantième Perpétuel skeleton ref. 25636BA, each estimated at £80,000 to £120,000 (about $104,500 to $156,700 USD), per Bonhams.

**My take:** the Daytona will grab the headlines, but HS C13 is the lot collectors will talk about. It is the start of the British military diver line that led to the MilSub and the CWC, and the estimate is modest for something this rare. Watch for whether it clears the top estimate on December 10.
