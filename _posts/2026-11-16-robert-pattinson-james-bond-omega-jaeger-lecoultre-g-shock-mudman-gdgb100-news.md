The biggest casting job in movies just got filled, and watch people everywhere had the same first thought: what is on his wrist? Today [Amazon MGM Studios announced](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/james-bond-robert-pattinson-cast-amazon-mgm-film) that **Robert Pattinson** will play James Bond in its next 007 film, directed by Denis Villeneuve from a script by Steven Knight, with Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/new-james-bond-4545b257a9924895a69623332db50c97) calls him the seventh actor to take the role, following Daniel Craig. "To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement," Pattinson said in the studio's announcement.

For the watch world, this one comes with a real plot twist, and I want to be clear up front about what is fact and what is guesswork. The short version: nobody has said which watch Pattinson's Bond will wear. Not Amazon MGM, not Omega, not Jaeger-LeCoultre. Everything below separates the confirmed pieces from the open questions.

## What is confirmed

* **Pattinson is Bond.** Announced by Amazon MGM Studios on November 16, 2026, and on the official 007 Instagram account.
* **He is a Jaeger-LeCoultre global ambassador.** [Jaeger-LeCoultre's press office announced him](https://press.jaeger-lecoultre.com/introducing-robert-pattinson/) as its newest global ambassador on September 2, 2026.
* **Omega has been Bond's watch for three decades.** As [Monochrome's history of the partnership](https://monochrome-watches.com/omega-seamaster-diver-300m-007-edition-210-90-42-20-01-001-review-price/) lays out, Omega has supplied 007's official timepiece since GoldenEye in 1995.
* **His first Bond imagery shows no watch.** [Fratello noticed](https://www.fratellowatches.com/robert-pattinson-confirmed-as-the-next-james-bond-no-watch-in-sight-for-now/) that in the black tie image and teaser shared by @007, Pattinson's wrist is bare.

## What is not confirmed

Whether Omega continues as Bond's watch under Amazon MGM, whether Jaeger-LeCoultre gets a role in the film, and what model Bond wears on screen. Fans are already arguing about it in Fratello's comments, with one reader betting on a blue Seamaster Professional and another predicting JLC takes over. Those are opinions, not reporting. Until a studio or a brand says something official, treat any "Bond's next watch" headline you see this week as speculation.

## Why this matters so much to watch fans

Bond is the single most powerful piece of product placement watchmaking has ever had. When Pierce Brosnan wore a blue dial Seamaster in GoldenEye, Monochrome notes that costume designer Lindy Hemming picked it because she believed a naval commander and diver would wear one. That quartz Seamaster, and the automatic reference 2531.80 that followed in Tomorrow Never Dies, turned the Seamaster Professional into one of the most recognized dive watches on the planet. If you own a 2531.80 today, my [strap guide for the Seamaster 300M 2531.80](https://mrtictoctalk.com/strap-finder/omega-seamaster-300m-2531-80/) covers what fits its 20 mm lugs.

Daniel Craig's era pushed it further. For No Time to Die, Omega built the **Seamaster Diver 300M 007 Edition**, reference **210.90.42.20.01.001**, a titanium, no date, military inspired take on the Diver 300M that Monochrome confirmed was not a limited edition. ## The Jaeger-LeCoultre angle

Here is why people are buzzing. Pattinson's JLC deal is barely two months old. Brands sign ambassadors to wear their watches in public, and Bond's watch has been a paid Omega partnership for as long as most of us have been watching these films. Something has to give, or both deals live side by side, with Pattinson wearing JLC on red carpets and Omega on screen. We simply don't know, and I won't pretend to. What I will say is that a new Bond, a new studio and a new director is exactly the moment when long partnerships get renegotiated, so this is a story to watch.

## Also new today: G-Shock Mudman GDGB100, $250 USD

From a tuxedo to the trenches. [Casio America announced](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/g-shock-toughness-gets-dirty-the-new-mudman-gdgb100-is-built-for-the-field-and-the-finish-line-302939317.html) the **G-Shock Mudman GDGB100** this morning, the newest member of the mud resistant Mudman line that started with the DW8400 back in 1995. This one is built for people who work in the dirt and then go train, and it brings a big screen upgrade.

* **Price:** $250 USD for each of three colorways: GDGB100-1 black, GDGB100-1A3 dark green, GDGB100-1A5 tan
* **Display:** memory in pixel (MIP) LCD for sharp readability in direct sunlight, plus a red auto LED backlight that's easier on your night vision
* **Build:** a center case sandwiched between a multifaceted front bezel and a rear bumper bezel, carbon fiber reinforced resin, gasketed buttons to keep grit out, and bio based resin in the case, bezel and band
* **Water resistance:** 200 m
* **On the watch:** distance, speed, pace and calories, step tracker, 1 second stopwatch with 100 lap memory, interval timer with up to five settings, vibration alerts, world time for 38 cities plus UTC, four alarms
* **With the Casio Watches app:** automatic time sync, about 300 cities of world time, distance calibration from your phone's GPS, training logs, phone notifications and phone finder
* **Size (per [G-Central's spec sheet](https://www.g-central.com/specs/g-shock-gdg-b100/)):** 63.6 x 49.6 x 16.2 mm, 66 g, module 3587, CR2032 battery rated for about two years, mineral crystal

That is a big watch, so keep the dimensions in mind if you have a smaller wrist. G-Central reports it launched in Japan and Asia in October before today's US announcement. Casio says it is available at select retailers, gshock.casio.com and the G-Shock Soho store.

My take: $250 for a Mudman with an MIP screen, a step counter and vibration alerts is strong value, and the tan version is the one I'd grab. It is a real tool for anyone who works outside and still wants their training data without wearing a fragile smartwatch.
