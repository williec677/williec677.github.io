While everybody else spent the long weekend chasing doorbusters, Citizen quietly dropped one of the best looking sub $1,500 automatics of the season on its US site. The Series 8 831, the octagonal, sharply faceted sport watch that sits between the Tsuyosa and The Citizen in the lineup, now comes in four limited editions with gradient textured dials. And riding along with them is something much weirder and much cooler: a Series 8 880 GMT in gold Damascus steel with a forged carbon dial, so no two are alike. Let's break it all down.

## What's new: four Series 8 831 limited editions

**US launch:** listed on [Citizen US](https://citizenwatch.com/us/en/product/NB6050-19W) this week, covered by [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/citizen-series-8-831-automatic-limited-editions-2026-nb6050-nb6059-nb6052-introducing-price/), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Citizen-launches-its-limited-edition-Series-8-automatics-in-the-US-including-a-GMT-model.1458678.0.html) and [The Gadgeteer](https://the-gadgeteer.com/2026/11/25/citizen-series8-831-gradient-dial-limited/)

![Citizen Series 8 831 NB6050-19W with a magenta gradient dial and NB6050-01L with a blue gradient dial]({{ "/assets/img/watches/citizen-series-8-831-limited-editions-880-gmt-damascus-news.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Citizen (NB6050-19W, left, and NB6050-01L)*

Notebookcheck reports that Citizen US first teased these on Instagram in September and that two of them launched in Hong Kong in October. Now all four are on Citizen's American store. Every one uses the same stamped dial texture Citizen used on last year's ice blue limited edition, a faceted pattern Monochrome says the brand ties to sunlight bouncing off clear ocean water. What changes is the color, the case finish and how loud you want to go:

* **NB6050-01L, blue:** steel case, gradient blue dial, blue leather strap. The easy everyday pick. **700 pieces, $1,125 USD**
* **NB6050-19W, magenta:** steel case, gradient magenta dial that Monochrome describes as magenta to burgundy, matching leather strap. The smallest run of the four. **600 pieces, $1,125 USD**
* **NB6059-06W, brown with rose gold tone bezel:** steel case with a rose gold tone bezel and crown, gradient brown dial with rose gold tone hands and markers, brown leather strap. **700 pieces, $1,150 USD**
* **NB6052-56W, amber brown on bracelet:** case and integrated H link bracelet finished in mixed yellow and rose gold tones, gradient amber brown dial. The full flex. **650 pieces, $1,295 USD**

All four are unnumbered limited editions, per Citizen. That's 2,650 watches total worldwide.

### Specs

* **Case:** 40 mm octagonal stainless steel, 11.6 mm thick per Monochrome, brushed and polished facets, gold tone coatings on the NB6059-06W bezel and the full NB6052-56W
* **Crystal:** anti reflective sapphire, with a display caseback
* **Water resistance:** 100 m
* **Lug width:** 20 mm (listed by Citizen for the NB6059-06W)
* **Movement:** in house Citizen caliber 9051 automatic, 4 Hz (28,800 vph), 24 jewels, 42 hours of power reserve, hacking seconds, claimed accuracy of minus 10 to plus 20 seconds a day (per Monochrome)
* **Magnetic resistance:** Citizen calls it enhanced magnetic resistance; Notebookcheck puts it at 16,000 A/m
* **Strap or bracelet:** leather with a push button deployant clasp, or the integrated two tone H link bracelet on the NB6052-56W

**Price:** all four prices above are **Citizen's own US prices** as listed on citizenwatch.com. For reference, Monochrome lists European pricing from EUR 890 to EUR 1,090.

## The wildcard: Series 8 880 GMT NB6092-01E in Damascus steel

This is the one that made me stop scrolling. The [Series 8 880 GMT NB6092-01E](https://citizenwatch.com/us/en/product/NB6092-01E) puts a 41 mm case in gold Damascus steel and black tone steel around a forged carbon dial. Damascus steel and forged carbon both have patterns that change from piece to piece, and Citizen says that makes each watch "uniquely its own." Notebookcheck notes the 24 hour bezel is black and brown.

* **Case:** 41 mm gold Damascus and black tone stainless steel, sapphire crystal, 100 m
* **Movement:** Citizen caliber 9054 automatic GMT, 50 hours of power reserve, date at 3
* **Strap:** grey leather with a fold over clasp
* **Edition:** 1,400 unnumbered pieces
* **Price:** **$2,195 USD** (Citizen US price)

## Why this matters

Citizen's mechanical game has gotten seriously good, and the Series 8 is where it shows. You get a sapphire crystal, 100 m of water resistance, magnetic resistance and an in house automatic for barely over a thousand dollars, in a case with more angles than most watches twice the price. The obvious rival is the integrated bracelet crowd led by the Tissot PRX, which we broke down in our [Tissot PRX Powermatic 80 buyer's guide]({% post_url 2026-11-04-tissot-prx-powermatic-80-buyers-guide %}). The PRX wins on power reserve with its 80 hours, but none of the PRX dials has the color drama these Series 8 gradients bring.

The honest knock is that 42 hour power reserve. Leave the 831 on the dresser over a long weekend and you'll be resetting it Monday morning. And with only 600 to 700 of each color, you shouldn't expect discounts anytime soon.

**My take:** the magenta NB6050-19W is the move. It's the rarest of the four, the color is unlike anything else at this price, and on a 20 mm strap it's easy to swap for a black or grey leather when you want to tone it down. If your taste runs bolder, the 880 GMT in Damascus steel is the most interesting watch Citizen has put out this month, and the fact that every case pattern is different makes it a genuine conversation piece for $2,195 USD. Citizen has been on a tear lately, from the [Attesa Ever Since titanium moonphase]({% post_url 2026-11-25-gaga-labormatic-arabia-akhor-stone-dials-citizen-attesa-ever-since-news %}) to the [Attesa Hazy limited editions]({% post_url 2026-11-20-nodus-oracle-time-sector-ii-red-jasper-alpina-galaga-citizen-attesa-hazy-news %}).
