Hand engraving from the Netherlands, a $299 hand wound field watch with a sense of humor and a Swiss Made Kickstarter that dresses like a poison dart frog. Today's second batch of news covers every corner of the hobby, from a 25 piece artisan run to an under $300 impulse buy. Let's go.

## Holthinrichs Signature Ornament Heritage Hand Engraved

**Announced:** November 24, 2026 ([Holthinrichs](https://holthinrichs.com/collection/signature-ornament-heritage-engraved/), [Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/holthinrichs-signature-ornament-hand-engraved), [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/holthinrichs-signature-ornament-heritage-hand-engraved-collection-new-hand-applied-dial-textures-review-price/), [Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/holthinrichs-signature-ornament-heritage-hand-engraved/))

![The four Holthinrichs Signature Ornament Heritage Hand Engraved editions side by side]({{ "/assets/img/watches/holthinrichs-heritage-hand-engraved-timex-ww-protocol-001.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Holthinrichs (from left: 18k yellow gold, 18k rose gold, white rhodium and black rhodium dial finishes)*

Architect Michiel Holthinrichs founded his Delft based brand in 2016 and made its name with sculptural 3D printed cases. For the 10th anniversary he is adding a craft the brand never had in house: hand engraving. The engraver is Camille Jean-Mairet, who trained for four years in hand engraving at the École d'art in La Chaux-de-Fonds, engraved watches for Bovet and now works in the Holthinrichs atelier as a movement finisher and engraver.

### The dials

Each dial is German silver, built in layers: a recessed small seconds subdial with raillures, a hand engraved center and a raised, circular brushed outer ring carrying hour numerals in a new Art Nouveau font Michiel drew himself (Hodinkee calls it an edgier take on Breguet numerals). A diamond tooled chamfer frames the center, and Holthinrichs says every dial takes about six hours of engraving. Each pattern gets its own galvanic finish (pairings per Monochrome and Hodinkee):

* **18k yellow gold:** dragon scales
* **18k rose gold (salmon):** a pattern Monochrome calls "tiger claws"
* **Black rhodium:** "hagelslag," the Dutch chocolate sprinkles
* **White rhodium:** free engraved organic lines that Holthinrichs has not named

New leaf shaped hands are split down the middle, half high polish and half frosted, so one side always catches the light.

### Specs (from Holthinrichs)

* **Case:** CNC machined grade 5 titanium with brushed and RAW finishes, 38.5 mm wide, 9.85 mm thick with the sapphire crystal (7.8 mm without), 46 mm lug to lug
* **Weight:** 37.7 g without strap
* **Water resistance:** 5 ATM (50 m)
* **Movement:** modified Sellita SW300 automatic with a custom Holthinrichs rotor, visible through a flat sapphire caseback. Outlets disagree on the details (Monochrome lists the SW360 small seconds version with 56 hours and 31 jewels, Fratello the SW300-1 with 50 hours and 25 jewels), so we are sticking with the brand's own wording until Holthinrichs publishes the full movement data.
* **Strap:** waxed leather with handmade micro stitching, 20/16 mm, in Cognac Brown, Navy Blue or Forest Green, with a titanium pin buckle

**Price and availability:** **about $7,564 USD (EUR 6,900)** excluding VAT and including international shipping, converted at the ECB reference rate of November 24, 2026 (EUR 1 = $1.0963). Four editions of 25 pieces, 100 in total, reserved through Holthinrichs' own site. Because of the hand work, the brand says the first 30 orders will be completed before June 2027 and the rest over the course of 2027, first come, first served.

**My take:** six hours of hand engraving on every dial is a lot of genuine craft for the money, and 38.5 mm with a 46 mm lug to lug is a perfect daily size. The yellow gold dragon scale is my pick. ## Timex x Worn & Wound Field Watch w/a Twist: Sunburst and Mist

**Announced:** November 24, 2026 ([Worn & Wound / Windup Watch Shop](https://windupwatchshop.com/pages/the-timex-x-worn-wound-field-watch-w-a-twist-now-in-sunburst-and-mist), [Timex](https://timex.com/blogs/the-timex-blog/worn-wound-x-timex-meet-sunburst-mist))

![Timex x Worn & Wound Field Watch w/a Twist in Sunburst and Mist on fabric straps]({{ "/assets/img/watches/timex-worn-wound-field-watch-twist-sunburst-mist.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Timex (Sunburst TW2Y89600 left, Mist TW2Y89700 right)*

Worn & Wound introduced this design with Timex last year, and now it comes back in two new colors. Quick disclosure on the source: Worn & Wound is a watch publication that also sells this collaboration through its own Windup Watch Shop, so the specs below come from both Worn & Wound and Timex.

The twist is the dial. It splits a classic field layout into three rings like a sector dial: a small 13 to 24 hour track in the middle, bold italic 1 to 12 hour numerals in pad printed C3 lume around it, and a minute track on the outside, all separated by railroad tracks.

* **Sunburst (TW2Y89600):** three shades of orange tinged red on the dial, white tracks over blue grey, seafoam blue hour and minute hands, a white seconds hand with a cyan tip and a matching teal fabric strap
* **Mist (TW2Y89700):** aqua, blue grey and teal rings with burgundy track rings, pale yellow lume numerals, a deep purple seconds hand with a pink tip and a sand colored fabric strap

### Specs

* **Case:** brushed steel barrel shape, 37 mm wide, 43 mm lug to lug, 12.2 mm thick (8 mm without the crystal), 20 mm lugs
* **Crystal:** domed acrylic
* **Movement:** Sea-Gull TY6, hand wound
* **Water resistance:** 50 m
* **Edition:** 578 pieces per color

**Price and availability:** **$299 USD** each, available now from the Windup Watch Shop and Timex, shipping immediately.

**My take:** a hand wound mechanical with this much personality for $299 USD is the easiest gift pick of the week. Mist is the one I would wear more, Sunburst is the one I would post. ## Protocol 001 Aposematic Collection on Kickstarter

**Launched:** November 24, 2026, 9:00 AM ET (3:00 PM CET) on [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/protocolwatches/protocol-001-the-aposematic-watch-collection) ([Protocol Watches](https://protocolwatches.com/))

![Protocol 001 Steel, Phantom and Carbon watch heads side by side]({{ "/assets/img/watches/protocol-001-aposematic-steel-phantom-carbon.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Protocol Watches (from left: Steel, Phantom, Carbon)*

Protocol is a new microbrand shipping from Konstanz, Germany, and its first collection is named after aposematism, the bright warning colors of poison dart frogs. Three Swiss Made versions, each limited to 100 numbered pieces:

* **Steel:** white sandblasted dial, steel case, blue accents
* **Phantom:** black dial, black PVD case, yellow accents
* **Carbon:** forged carbon dial, black PVD case, red accents

### Specs (from Protocol's product pages)

* **Case:** 316L stainless steel (black PVD on Phantom and Carbon), 38.5 mm wide, 14 mm thick, 47.3 mm lug to lug, 20 mm lugs, screw down crown
* **Crystal:** sapphire with inner antireflective coating
* **Movement:** Swiss Sellita SW200 automatic, 26 jewels, 4 Hz, about 38 hours, hacking, no date
* **Water resistance:** 100 m

**Price:** after Kickstarter, Protocol lists **Steel at about $1,058 USD (EUR 965)**, **Phantom at about $1,123 USD (EUR 1,024)** and **Carbon at about $1,181 USD (EUR 1,077)**, all before VAT and shipping, at the ECB rate of November 24, 2026 (EUR 1 = $1.0963). The Kickstarter early bird takes 25% off for the first 25 pieces per color, starting at **about $794 USD (EUR 724)** for the Steel. Shipping to the US and Canada is EUR 45 (about $49 USD), and US backers handle any import duties.

**What we could not confirm:** Kickstarter blocked our reader from loading the full reward list, so check the campaign page for the exact pledge tiers and the delivery estimate, which Protocol's site does not state. Protocol's product pages list a 2 year international warranty while its FAQ says 1 year, and the strap options also differ between the two (rubber, leather or Cordura versus leather and textile), so confirm both before you back. As with any crowdfunded watch, a pledge is not a guaranteed delivery date.

**My take:** a Swiss Made SW200 automatic at about $794 USD early bird is fair money, and the Carbon is a genuine head turner. At 14 mm thick on a 38.5 mm case it will wear chunky, and first time brands always carry risk.
