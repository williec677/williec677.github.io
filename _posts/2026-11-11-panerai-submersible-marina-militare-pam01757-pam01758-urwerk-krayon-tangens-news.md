Panerai built its legend on Italian Navy tools, and this morning it leaned all the way back into that story. The **Submersible Marina Militare Destro PAM01757** and **Submersible Marina Militare GMT PAM01758** are two brushed steel divers with matte black ceramic bezels, beige lume and 500 m of water resistance, and they are available now. Below that, three smaller stories worth your coffee: a 50 piece **Urwerk UR-101 Green Mamba**, a hand painted **Krayon Anywhere Magnolia** in platinum, and a new **Tangens** Storm capsule for collectors on a budget. Specs come from Monochrome, Fratello, Hodinkee and Panerai's own product copy, and US prices come from authorized dealer listings, all checked on November 11, 2026.

## Panerai Submersible Marina Militare PAM01757 Destro and PAM01758 GMT

Panerai's link to the Marina Militare goes back to the 1910s, when it supplied the Italian Navy with depth gauges and compasses. The Submersible family is much younger. As [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/panerai-submersible-marina-militare-gmt-pam01758-and-submersible-marina-militare-destro-pam01757-military-dive-watche-video-review-price/) points out, it started in 1998 with the PAM00024, and its signature is the unidirectional rotating bezel. What makes this pair special is that bezel insert: matte black ceramic, which Monochrome notes is still fairly rare on a Submersible and echoes the PAM00389 from 2012.

The US prices are what Panerai authorized dealer [The 1916 Company lists for the PAM01757](https://www.the1916company.com/watches/panerai/submersible/pam01757/) and [for the PAM01758](https://www.the1916company.com/watches/panerai/submersible/pam01758/). The euro prices come from Monochrome and [Fratello](https://www.fratellowatches.com/new-panerai-submersible-marina-militare-pam01757-destro-and-pam01758-gmt/), and the conversions in brackets use today's rate just so you can compare. These are brand new, so there are no secondary market sales yet.

Monochrome reports that the Destro's solid caseback is engraved with the Siluro a Lenta Corsa, the slow running torpedo that Italian Navy frogmen rode in World War II, and that both watches carry the words "Annual Production" on the back. Monochrome reads that as a yearly production cap rather than a numbered limited edition, so don't expect a serial like 001/500.

### The Destro: the clean one

Destro means right in Italian, and on a Panerai it means the whole crown guard assembly moves to the left of the case. Fratello's Jorg Weppelink says that is exactly why it works, because Panerai flips the full bridge instead of just moving the crown, and on a right handed wearer the crown stops digging into the back of your hand. Dropping the date keeps the dial calm and symmetrical. At 44 mm this is still a big watch, but fully brushed steel and a matte black bezel make it look more like a tool than jewelry.

### The GMT: the useful one

The PAM01758 is two millimeters smaller across and a touch thicker. Its GMT is the caller or office style, meaning the 24 hour hand is the one you set on its own, so it is built to track home time while the main hands stay local. Fratello notes one quirk: changing either time means unscrewing the crown under that big lever, which is a small extra step on travel days. The 24 hour track lives on the flange so the dial stays readable.

### My take

This is the most Panerai thing Panerai has done all year, and I mean that as a compliment. Beige lume and a brown leather strap on a 500 m diver sounds like it shouldn't work, but it reads as pure military heritage. If you want one watch, I would take the GMT: 42 mm wears on more wrists, and a dive watch that also tracks a second time zone covers almost everything. If you are a lefty, or just want the cleanest dial in the lineup, the Destro is a rare thing in the big brand world. ## Urwerk UR-101 Green Mamba: 50 pieces

The UR-101 is where Urwerk's wandering hours started in 1997, and [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/urwerk-ur-101-green-mamba-black-pvd-wandering-hour-watch-introducing-price/) reports today's version keeps decoration to a minimum and lets color do the talking. Two hour satellites ride a carousel and sweep across a 180 degree emerald green minute track, so the number you see tells the hour and its position tells the minutes. The case is 41 mm by 10.3 mm in black PVD coated steel, with 30 m of water resistance and an anti glare metallised sapphire crystal. Inside is the automatic UR-1.01V caliber on a Vaucher base, 28,800 vph and 48 hours of reserve. It comes on black rubber with a black DLC steel buckle. **Only 50 pieces, CHF 33,000 before tax, about $40,070 USD.** Urwerk did not publish a US price.

## Krayon Anywhere Magnolia: 15 pieces of hand painted flowers

The Neuchâtel independent [Krayon](https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/krayon-magnolia) has a new métiers d'art version of its Anywhere, the watch that shows sunrise and sunset for any location a watchmaker sets. According to Hodinkee, this one uses a black onyx dial with falling magnolia petals hand painted by an artist in Neuchâtel, so each of the 15 is slightly different, inside a 39 mm by 9.5 mm platinum case. The hand wound in house caliber C030 runs at 3 Hz with 72 hours of reserve. **15 pieces at CHF 178,000, about $216,137 USD,** through Krayon retailers. Monochrome and Deployant covered it as well.

## Tangens Komposition Automatic Storm: the budget pick

Danish brand Tangens, founded in 2023, added two new dials to its 1970s inspired Komposition Automatic. [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/the-accessible-tangens-komposition-automatic-storm-collection-in-dusty-blue-dusty-purple-first-look-price/), in a post it labels as sponsored by Tangens, lists the new Dusty Blue and Dusty Purple dials in a smooth matte finish. Specs stay the same: 38 mm steel case, 10.45 mm thick, 45.5 mm lug to lug, sapphire, 100 m, a single link bracelet with quick release bars and a Miyota 9039 automatic with about 42 hours of reserve and no date. **EUR 469 including tax, about $525 USD,** on the brand's site now. For a slim, no date sports watch with a real bracelet, that is a fun entry point, and fans of independents who want something under four figures should also look at the [Albishorn Voisin Aérosport]({% assign al = site.posts | where: "slug", "albishorn-voisin-aerosport-jlc-collectibles-angelier-news" | first %}{{ al.url | absolute_url }}) roundup for the Angelier Kickstarter dress watch.
