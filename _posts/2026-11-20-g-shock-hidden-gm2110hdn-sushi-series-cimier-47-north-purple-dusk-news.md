Friday menu, and two of the three courses come from Casio. G-Shock just confirmed a steel bezel collaboration with New York's HIDDEN, its made in Japan Sushi Series has finally crossed the Pacific to US stores, and over in Biel a 102 year old Swiss brand gave its affordable field watch a very loud new dial. Let's get into it.

## G-Shock x HIDDEN GM2110HDN-8A

**Announced:** November 19, 2026 ([Casio America press release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/casio-releases-g-shock-collaboration-watch-with-hidden-302944465.html), [G-Central](https://www.g-central.com/hidden-x-g-shock-gm2110hdn-8a-celebrates-new-york-city-street-culture/))

![G-Shock x HIDDEN GM2110HDN-8A in silver with steel bezel and bracelet worn on four wrists holding a subway pole]({{ "/assets/img/watches/g-shock-hidden-gm2110hdn-8a.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Casio America (ref. GM2110HDN-8A)*

HIDDEN, the New York online collective and lifestyle brand that grew out of a massive Instagram following, has worked with G-Shock before on square DW5600 models. This time Casio moved the partnership up to the octagonal GM2110, the version with the metal bezel, and according to G-Central it is the first HIDDEN collab built on that case.

What Casio's own release confirms:

* **Look:** an urban silver color scheme with HIDDEN's signature green on the 3, 6, 9 and 12 o'clock indexes.
* **Logo:** the inset subdial at 9 o'clock carries HIDDEN's "h" logo.
* **New York details:** the Statue of Liberty is engraved on both the buckle and the caseback, and the watch ships in packaging designed around HIDDEN's visual identity.

G-Central adds a few more details: a stainless steel band, "Past, Present & Future" and "Manhattan New York" on the caseback in English and Japanese, a deluxe tin case and box art inspired by the souvenir shops on Canal Street. G-Central also calls it a limited edition; Casio's release does not give a production number, so we can't confirm how many will be made.

**Price and release:** **$385 USD**, on sale **November 25, 2026** at gshock.casio.com, the G-Shock SoHo Store in New York and select retailers.

**My take:** HIDDEN's earlier G-Shocks were $170 to $175 resin DW5600s, so this one is a real step up in materials and price. The silver and green look is subtle enough to wear with anything, and the Statue of Liberty on the clasp is the kind of detail you notice every time you take it off. If you are hunting collabs, also see the [G-Shock x Sneaker Politics Oyster DW6900 we covered this week]({% post_url 2026-11-17-union-glashutte-averin-date-g-shock-sneaker-politics-oyster-news %}).

## G-Shock Sushi Series is now in the US

**US launch reported:** November 18, 2026 ([G-Central](https://www.g-central.com/japan-made-g-shock-sushi-series-now-available-in-u-s/), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Casio-s-Japan-made-sushi-themed-G-Shocks-finally-land-in-the-US.1452768.0.html), [Hypebeast](https://hypebeast.com/2026/11/casio-g-shock-sushi-series-watches-ga100ssh-4a-dw5600ssh-4-dw6900ssh-1-release-info))

![G-Shock Sushi Series GA-2100SSH-4A, DW-5600SSH-4 and DW-6900SSH-1 on a sushi tray]({{ "/assets/img/watches/g-shock-sushi-series.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Casio (left to right: GA-2100SSH-4A, DW-5600SSH-4, DW-6900SSH-1)*

These three first went on sale in Japan in October and reached Europe in early November. Now they are listed in the US, and they are some of the most fun G-Shocks of the year. All three are made at Casio's Yamagata factory in Japan, and each one is a different piece of sushi:

* **GA-2100SSH-4A, salmon, $145 USD:** a bright orange, marbled case and dial that mimics fatty salmon, hour and minute hands shaped like kitchen knives, and a small inset dial at 9 o'clock shaped like a piece of nigiri.
* **DW-5600SSH-4, tuna, $155 USD:** translucent red resin case and band, a dial patterned after lean tuna, a wasabi green accent and a little tuna silhouette.
* **DW-6900SSH-1, ikura, $165 USD:** based on ikura gunkan maki, with a black bezel for the seaweed wrap, glossy salmon roe graphics over a translucent dial and a white band underside standing in for the rice.

Shared details: every caseback is engraved with a yunomi teacup, the cup of tea served at the end of a sushi meal, and the watches keep standard G-Shock specs including 200 m of water resistance and a mineral crystal. The box is folded like a sushi takeaway container, and when you lift the watch out you find a hidden wasabi graphic. Casio scaled the wasabi to the fish: a little for salmon, a lot for tuna and none for ikura. G-Central describes all three as limited edition models; no production numbers are published.

**Price:** $145, $155 and $165 USD, available now through Casio's US webstore and authorized retailers.

**My take:** themed G-Shocks can feel like novelty, but the knife hands, the teacup caseback and the packaging show real thought. The salmon GA-2100 is the pick for me. ## Also new: Cimier 47° North Purple Dusk

**Announced:** November 20, 2026 ([Cimier](https://www.cimier.com/watches/47-north-purple), [Monochrome](https://monochrome-watches.com/cimier-47-degree-north-purple-dusk-accessible-automatic-field-watch-review-price/))

![Cimier 47° North Purple Dusk with grained purple gradient dial, old radium Arabic numerals and light blue seconds hand on a black leather strap]({{ "/assets/img/watches/cimier-47-north-purple-dusk.webp" | relative_url }})
*Image: Cimier (47° North Purple Dusk)*

Cimier was founded in 1924 and is now based in Biel, a city that sits at 47 degrees north, which is where the field watch gets its name. The 47° North already comes in black, white, grey, green and blue; Purple Dusk is the newest color, inspired by the last light over the Swiss Alps. The dial keeps the collection's grained texture with a gradient, printed Arabic numerals in old radium Super-LumiNova and a light blue seconds hand with a lume block.

Specs from Cimier's product page:

* **Case:** 316L steel, 38 mm, 46 mm lug to lug, 20 mm lugs, box sapphire crystal, screw down crown, 100 m water resistance. Cimier lists 10.95 mm thick including the crystal, while Monochrome reports 11.25 mm, so we are going with the brand's number.
* **Movement:** ETA 2824-2 automatic, 38 hours of power reserve. Monochrome adds 28,800 vph, 25 jewels, a hacking seconds function, Geneva stripes, blued screws and a custom rotor with an orange cutout, visible through a sapphire caseback.
* **Strap options:** black calf leather with pin buckle, or a steel bracelet with a butterfly clasp. Monochrome notes quick release spring bars.
* **Availability:** permanent collection, available now per Monochrome.

**Price:** **CHF 850** on leather, about **$1,009 USD**, and **CHF 990** on the steel bracelet, about **$1,175 USD**, as listed on Cimier's store. Monochrome describes those prices as excluding taxes. Cimier has not published a US dollar price.

**My take:** an ETA 2824 field watch with a box sapphire, a screw down crown and 100 m for around a thousand dollars is a fair Swiss deal, and 38 mm with 46 mm lug to lug fits almost any wrist. The purple is bold, but the old radium lume keeps it from looking like a toy.
