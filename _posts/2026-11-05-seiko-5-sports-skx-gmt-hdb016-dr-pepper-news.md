Collectors love naming GMT bezels after soda, and Seiko just poured a new one. The 5 Sports SKX GMT U.S. Limited Edition, reference HDB016, wraps a deep maroon sunray dial in a red and dark brown 24 hour bezel, and it is already being called the "Dr Pepper." It's limited to 5,500 numbered pieces, it's U.S. only, and it costs $525 retail. I checked every spec and price below against named sources on November 5, 2026.

## Where the "Dr Pepper" name comes from

Seiko didn't name it, so let's be precise. Retailer spec sheets describe the dial as "inspired by a popular soft drink brand" ([Eli Adams](https://eliadam.com/products/hdb016)). His team's launch article says the color "recalls the side of a Dr Pepper can" and places it next to fan nicknames like the "Pepsi" SSK019 and the "Root Beer" SSK036 ([Teddy Baldassarre](https://teddybaldassarre.com/blogs/watches/seiko-skx-hdb016-dr-pepper)). Montre24 reports that the colors have "already earned it the nickname" online ([Montre24](https://montre24.com/news/2026-11-03/4487/)), and Notebookcheck ran with the same comparison ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Seiko-s-new-limited-edition-5-Sports-GMT-looks-like-a-Dr-Pepper-can.1435824.0.html)). So it's a community and media nickname, not an official Seiko name.

## The dial and bezel

This is all about color. The maroon dial has a sunray finish, so it shifts from wine red to almost brown as it catches light. The GMT hand is gold, and it matches a golden sloped chapter ring carrying the 24 hour scale. The bidirectional bezel splits red for daytime and dark brown for night, and in some light that brown reads almost black ([Teddy Baldassarre](https://teddybaldassarre.com/blogs/watches/seiko-skx-hdb016-dr-pepper)). LumiBrite fills the hands and markers, and there's a date at 3 o'clock.

## Case, specs, and movement

The case is the familiar SKX GMT shape with the 4 o'clock crown. Per Teddy Baldassarre's launch article, it measures 42.5mm across, 46mm lug to lug and 13.6mm thick, with a 22mm lug width ([Teddy Baldassarre](https://teddybaldassarre.com/blogs/watches/seiko-skx-hdb016-dr-pepper)). One retailer, Carat & Co., lists the thickness as 13.4mm ([Carat & Co.](https://www.caratco.com/products/hdb016)), so expect roughly 13.5mm on the wrist. Water resistance is 100 meters, the screw down caseback is an exhibition back, and the crystal is Hardlex ([Carat & Co.](https://www.caratco.com/products/hdb016), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Seiko-s-new-limited-edition-5-Sports-GMT-looks-like-a-Dr-Pepper-can.1435824.0.html)).

Inside is the automatic caliber 4R34 with 24 jewels, beating at 21,600 vph, with about 41 hours of power reserve, hand winding and hacking seconds. It's a caller GMT, so the 24 hour hand sets on its own while local time stays put ([Teddy Baldassarre](https://teddybaldassarre.com/blogs/watches/seiko-skx-hdb016-dr-pepper)). It ships on a stainless steel bracelet with a tri fold clasp, push button release and security lock.

## Price, date, and where to buy

* **Reference:** HDB016, limited to 5,500 numbered pieces for the U.S. market.
* **Price:** $525 retail, the price listed by Carat & Co. and reported by Montre24 and Notebookcheck. One jeweler, Eli Adams, shows $625, so compare before you pay.
* **Dates:** Teddy Baldassarre published the announcement on October 27, 2026, and retailers list a November 2, 2026 release date.
* **Where:** Seiko boutiques and authorized U.S. retailers ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Seiko-s-new-limited-edition-5-Sports-GMT-looks-like-a-Dr-Pepper-can.1435824.0.html)).

I couldn't find an HDB016 product page on Seiko USA as of this morning, and Hodinkee, Monochrome, Fratello, Worn & Wound and aBlogtoWatch hadn't covered it yet either, so the specs here come from the retailer listings and the outlets above.

## How it fits the SSK family

The 5 Sports GMT line launched in July 2022 with the black SSK001, the blue SSK003 and the orange SSK005, and Seiko lists the SSK001 and SSK003 at $495 ([Seiko SSK001](https://www.seikowatches.com/us-en/products/5sports/ssk001), [Seiko SSK003](https://www.seikowatches.com/us-en/products/5sports/ssk003)). That means the Dr Pepper costs just $30 more than the core models for a numbered, U.S. only colorway. If 42.5mm is too big for you, the 39.4mm Field GMT SSK023 is $450 ([Seiko SSK023](https://www.seikowatches.com/us-en/products/5sports/ssk023)).

My take? This is one of the most fun GMTs you can buy under $600. The movement and case are proven, and the colors are the whole story, warm and a little retro, unlike anything else in the lineup. 5,500 pieces isn't tiny, but popular SSK specials have a habit of getting scarce, so if the color grabs you, buy at retail and skip the flippers.
