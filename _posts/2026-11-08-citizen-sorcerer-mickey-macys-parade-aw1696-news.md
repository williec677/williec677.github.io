Five hundred pieces. That is the entire run of Citizen's new Sorcerer Mickey's 100th Anniversary Macy's Parade watch, reference AW1696-03W, and it is built for the one parade every New Yorker has either stood on Central Park West for or watched from the couch with coffee. It costs $395, it runs on light, and it is sold in exactly two places: Macy's and [Citizen's US site](https://www.citizenwatch.com/us/en/product/AW1696-03W). I checked the price, the edition size, and every spec below against Citizen's own product page on November 8, 2026, when it was listed at $395 and still orderable.

## Mickey over Manhattan

The whole dial is one illustration, and Citizen commissioned it from Disney artists John and Shelley Loter. Mickey hangs from his balloon lines in the Sorcerer's Apprentice robe and hat, floating above the New York City skyline on a deep blue dial. Citizen points out that Mickey first appeared as a Macy's parade balloon in 1934, which is why he gets the starring role for the parade's centennial year.

What keeps it from turning into a cartoon sticker is restraint. Red and gold stars stand in for the hour markers, gold hands and a thin gold ring frame the art, and the date sits at 3 o'clock without fighting the picture. Citizen lists a metal dial with three hands and a date. It reads like a classic three hander first and a character watch second, and that is exactly the balance a collectible like this needs to hit if you want to actually wear it past December.

## The specs that matter

Here is what Citizen's product page lists for the AW1696-03W:

* Case: two tone stainless steel, 40 mm wide, 9.29 mm thick
* Crystal: sapphire
* Movement: Citizen J810 Eco-Drive, hours, minutes, seconds and date
* Water resistance: 50 m (WR50, 5 bar), rated for still water swimming
* Strap: blue leather with a buckle
* Edition: 500 pieces worldwide, edition number engraved on the caseback
* Packaging: a custom designed gift box
* Price and availability: $395, exclusive to Macy's and citizenwatch.com

That sapphire crystal is the quiet win here. Plenty of licensed character watches at this money ship with mineral glass, and sapphire means the artwork you are paying for stays clear of desk scratches for years. A 9.29 mm case is also slim enough to slide under a sweater cuff on Thanksgiving morning.

## Why Eco-Drive makes sense on a collectible

Citizen notes that Eco-Drive turns 50 this year, and on a watch like this the tech is not just a talking point. A light powered movement means no dead battery when you pull it out of the gift box next November, and no trip to a jeweler for a cell swap that risks the caseback you are supposed to keep pristine. Leave it near a window and it keeps running. For a piece many people will wear on special occasions and store the rest of the year, that is the right movement choice.

Citizen did not publish a lug width on the product page, so measure before you buy a spare strap. If you already wear Citizen, the [Citizen Tsuyosa strap guide](https://mrtictoctalk.com/strap-finder/citizen-tsuyosa/) in our Strap Finder shows how the brand's own straps and bracelets are set up on a modern 40 mm three hander, and the full [Watch Strap Finder](https://mrtictoctalk.com/strap-finder/) covers 76 models.

## Should you grab one?

My take: if the Macy's parade is part of your family's Thanksgiving, or you have a Disney fan on your gift list, this is an easy yes at $395. You get sapphire, a numbered caseback, a slim case and a movement that never needs a battery, wrapped around artwork made specifically for this edition. The parade itself steps off on Thanksgiving, November 26, 2026, and with only 500 pieces split between Macy's and one website, I would not wait for Black Friday hoping for a markdown.

Be honest with yourself about what it is, though. This is a licensed collectible with a quartz based solar movement, not a mechanical investment piece. Buy it because you love the dial.
