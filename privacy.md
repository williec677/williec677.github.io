_Last updated: November 2, 2026_

This policy explains what happens to information when you visit the MrTicTocTalk Journal (mrtictoctalk.com).

## The short version

The Journal has no accounts, no comments, and no contact forms. It does not sell personal information.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load a page, for security and to operate the service. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Affiliate links

Some links go to partner sites such as Amazon, eBay, or Chrono24 (through the Awin network). If you click one, that site and its affiliate network may set cookies to credit the referral. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

## Advertising

If advertising is enabled, the site uses Google AdSense. Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) and learn more at [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can also opt out of some third party vendors' cookies at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## Newsletter

If you subscribe to email updates, your email address is handled by the newsletter provider named on the signup page, under its privacy policy, and you can unsubscribe from any email. Subscribing by RSS shares nothing with this site.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated, and the date above will change when it is. For questions, send a DM to [@mrtictoctalk on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mrtictoctalk/).
