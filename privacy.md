_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

This policy explains what happens to information when you visit the MrTicTocTalk Journal (mrtictoctalk.com).

## The short version

The Journal has no accounts and no comments. It does not sell personal information. If you subscribe to the newsletter, Kit handles your email address as described below.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load a page, for security and to operate the service. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Affiliate links

Some links go to partner sites: Amazon (through Amazon Associates) and eBay (through the eBay Partner Network). If you click one, that site and its affiliate network may set cookies to credit the referral. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. As an eBay Partner, I may be compensated if you make a purchase through eBay links. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

## Newsletter

If you subscribe to The MrTicTocTalk Weekly Watch Report, your email address is handled by [Kit](https://kit.com/privacy), under Kit's privacy policy, and you can unsubscribe from any email. Subscribing by RSS shares nothing with this site.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated, and the date above will change when it is. For questions, send a DM to [@mrtictoctalk on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/mrtictoctalk/).
