_Last updated: November 3, 2026_

Some posts on this site contain affiliate links. I earn a commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. It helps keep the Journal free.

**Programs this site participates in:**

* **Amazon Associates.** Links to tools, straps, watch winders, and books may point to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
* **eBay Partner Network.** Links to eBay searches or listings, including the "See current listings on eBay" boxes in buying guides, are eBay Partner Network links. As an eBay Partner, I may be compensated if you make a purchase through eBay links.

**What affiliate links do not change:**

* Opinions are my own. No brand pays for coverage, and no brand reviews articles before publication.
* Affiliate links never decide what gets recommended. If a tool is junk, it does not get linked.
* Prices quoted in articles come from completed sales or published auction results with a source and date, never from an affiliate partner.

Links that earn a commission are marked with `rel="sponsored"` in the page code, and posts that may contain them show a short notice near the top.
