Watch ownership calculator

This calculator has no affiliate links, and nothing on this page is sponsored. The companion article contains affiliate links, disclosed at the top of that article.

This is a MrTicTocTalk True Cost Breakdown tool. Enter the price you actually pay, an estimated service cost, and the resale value you expect, all in US dollars. Add selling fees if you will pay them. The calculator returns an illustrative net ownership cost: purchase plus service, minus net resale after fees, plus hypothetical results if the watch sells 0, 10 or 20 percent below what you paid.

The result is not a prediction of future value, and it is not personalized buying advice. Taxes, shipping, extra parts, different service needs, and later market moves are left out, and selling fees are only included if you enter them. Your purchase price can dramatically change your ownership cost, so if you buy under retail, use the price you would actually pay.

Official US retail and official service prices were checked November 18, 2026, taxes excluded. WatchCharts estimated private sale prices and one year sales volume are as of November 17, 2026. Dealer expect to pay figures were not used.

Verified examples used in the video

Scenario 1 assumes you buy at official retail, pay one official complete service, and subtract today's estimated private sale price. Scenario 2 assumes you buy at that same estimated private sale price, pay one official complete service, and subtract the same estimate. Scenario 2 is a way to see what happens after the first owner's depreciation. Selling later at today's estimate is not a forecast.

Reported sales show market activity. They do not, by themselves, tell you how quickly an individual watch will sell.
