Watch service costs

A service bill is part of owning a watch. These guides only include brands that publish official complete service prices. If we cannot read a number from the brand, we leave that brand out rather than guess.

  • Rolex service costs

    Official US complete service prices, checked November 3, 2026. Modern sports models start at $770. Five digit sports models are $1,210. Vintage tiers run much higher.

    Open the Rolex guide

Brands not listed yet

Tudor describes its service process and says you receive an itemized estimate after inspection, but it does not publish a fixed price list. Omega has an official service price page, yet the US figures did not appear in a form we could verify without guessing, so Omega is left out for now. Seiko, Citizen, Tissot and Hamilton do not publish a public complete service price list that we could confirm.

If you own a Rolex, also see the longer journal writeup of the official list.
